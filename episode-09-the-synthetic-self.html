    Who is already speaking in the voices of people who cannot consent, who pays to keep a real identity coherent, and why is the first getting cheaper while the second never does?

    THE TWO LEDGERS ▾
    MOMENT 02 / 06 · PIPELINE
    TWO LEDGERS · ONE MARKET

    What is made of you. What it costs to stay you.

    The same market sells both. On the left, the replica: a debit against you, made for someone's purpose. On the right, the original: the rent you pay to remain recognizable, recoverable, continuous and, now, remembered.

    Debit ledger · the replica what is made of you

    01

    For someone else's purpose

    Campaign speech, pulpit audio, fraud calls. The engine is voice cloning at falling input cost. The persuasion evidence is measured, not mythic: in three experiments with 76,977 UK participants across 19 models, post training for persuasion raised persuasiveness by as much as 51%, and where it rose, factual accuracy fell. FACTstudy finding · Hackenburg et al., Science, Dec 2025 Points of attitude change, not possession.

    02

    For your own purpose

    Agents that schedule, negotiate and message as you. The convenience is real. The unsettled part is a binding decision you did not personally make, attributed to you.

    03

    For the people who survive you

    Griefbots and memorial avatars. The product is handed to the family. Where the voice goes when the company goes is the next episode.

    Rent ledger · the original what it costs to stay you

    07

    The cost of being recognized

    Verification and identity services that log you into banks, agencies and clinics, and sell you the assurance that it was you.

    08

    The cost of being recoverable

    Password managers, backups, takeover recovery. Paid for continuously; needed rarely; catastrophic when absent.

    09

    The cost of being continuous

    Every move, job change, name change and phone change is reconciliation labor across accounts that never agreed on who you are.

    10

    The cost of being remembered

    Companion and memorial products price intimacy in tiers. Replika's store sells "items that change your Replika's personality or appearance." FACTcompany documentation Character.AI's c.ai+ is $9.99 a month. REPORTED HereAfter's FAQ, asked what happens if a subscriber can no longer pay, says "an authorized family member could continue paying to maintain access"; the FAQ does not address death directly, and says customers may download their recordings if the company shuts down. FACTcompany documentation, read 8 Nov 2026

    The hinge of the episode: the rent ledger's fourth line is the debit ledger's third line, seen from the family's side. The bill for remembering you is paid by the people who loved you. INFERENCEeditorial frame

    THREE POPULATIONS ▾
    MOMENT 03 / 06 · OUTCOME
    THREE POPULATIONS

    The originals, the targets, the payers

    01 · The originals

    Their voices are inputs.

    Most never consented, because most were never asked. The Pelkey vacatur is the first appellate signal we have found that a court will separate "what the family believed he would say" from his own voice. REPORTEDone ruling, one state

    02 · The targets

    Voters, congregations, the grandparent on the phone.

    The FCC ruled on 8 February 2024 that AI generated voices are "artificial" under the TCPA, so such robocalls need prior express consent. FACTrule text By our tally of Public Citizen's tracker (21 Oct 2026), about 33 states have enacted election deepfake laws; the count moves with how Hawaii's struck law and Massachusetts's sunset are treated. FACTeditorial tally; the tracker states no count

    03 · The payers

    Households whose subscriptions lapse in a shock.

    The class the engine sorted you into decides which you can hold. Wages are the denominator (S1 · The Trickle). We found no population series on what households spend to stay coherent; the ledger below asks you instead. INFERENCEsearch result

    FIVE DEFENSES ▾
    MOMENT 04 / 06 · COMPETING
    SYSTEMS REALIZATION · ONE HALF · THE CASE FOR

    Five defenses, hosted at their strongest

    1

    Useful agency

    Agents free attention. For many users that is the whole story, and it is a true one.

    2

    Grief and advocacy

    The Shotline families chose the voices and labeled every call. Consent and disclosure are not an afterthought there; they are the design. REPORTED

    3

    Estate licensed use

    ElevenLabs' Iconic Marketplace requires rights holder approval per use. The Tupac estate forced a track down within about a day of its demand. REPORTED

    4

    Consent regimes are maturing

    Tennessee's ELVIS Act, California's AB 1836, New York's 40 year post mortem right, the FEC's technology neutral fraud rule (Sept 2024), and EU AI Act Article 50 labeling duties in force since 2 August 2026. FACTrule text REPORTEDstatute summaries

    5

    Subscriptions buy security

    Continuous patching and recovery cost money. Free tiers exist. Self managed identity fails more often for non experts. A subscription can be honest pricing for real work.

    How the defenses differ in authority, scale, duration and remedy
    DefenseAuthorityScaleDurationRemedy if it fails

    Fairness is not symmetry. The defenses share a premise, that a replica can be made rightly, and differ in who can enforce it and for how long. The only defense that covers everyone is the one that ends when the payment does.

    DISCIPLINE ▾
    MOMENT 05 / 06 · DISCIPLINE
    SYSTEMS REALIZATION · OTHER HALF · WHAT WE REFUSE AND WHAT WE DID NOT PROVE

    Four refusals

    1

    No mind control framing

    Persuasion effects are measured in points of attitude change. Systems shape the conditions under which a belief becomes more likely. They do not implant it.

    2

    No inevitability

    Every case on the wall was a choice by a named party, a family or a volunteer. The Shotline shows the same tool used with consent.

    3

    No pathologizing of consenting use

    A family that builds a labeled voice is not a cautionary tale. The Second Death found the outcome evidence thin in both directions.

    4

    No "racket" framing of subscriptions

    Security and recovery are real work. The question is who pays when the subscriber cannot, not whether anyone should be paid.

    What this episode did not prove

    • That any named platform built a specific synthetic without consent. WATCH
    • That a synthetic voice of a dead person changed any election outcome; no 2026 midterm use of a deceased person's voice was found through 6 Nov 2026. INFERENCEsearch result
    • That identity stack cost is rising faster than wages for most households; no population series found. WATCH
    • That companion or memorial products cause harm. WATCH

    What this episode did establish

    • Seven documented, dated uses of the voices or likenesses of the dead in politics, advocacy, worship and court, across three countries. REPORTED
    • That one vendor's stated input requirement for a credible replica fell from hours to seconds. REPORTEDcompany claim
    • That remembrance is priced in tiers, and that at least one memorial vendor's documentation makes continued access a family payment when the subscriber cannot pay. FACTcompany documentation
    • That post training can raise a model's persuasiveness while lowering its accuracy. FACTstudy finding

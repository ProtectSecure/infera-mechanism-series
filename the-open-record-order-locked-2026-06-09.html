Two doors to the same World Cup

The morning's quietest document is its loudest signal. A consular-fee rule published in today's Federal Register, and a single-source report from last week, describe the same mechanism from opposite ends: paid expedited access for some applicants, refusal for others, both anchored to the 2026 World Cup.

Why it matters
Access becomes a priced good at the exact moment access becomes a weapon. The fee rule gives the previously single-source visa story a primary-source spine: the same government, the same tournament, the same week, pricing expedited entry for some while reportedly refusing others. That is the access-and-money mechanism on the record. Confidence: the fee rule is FACT (published); the refusal count is REPORTED. Next checkpoint: any US or State confirmation of the refusals; the comment docket by July 9.

Act IV · Service Alerts, Pre-Written

If this happens, read this first.

Six branch notes written before the night so the explanation arrives before the surprise (from the board's prewrite scaffold, WATCH).

NC first drop leans one way, then shifts

Early vote reports first, Election Day batch later. Do not call on the first drop; cite the 2022 timing.

PA count runs slow

Philadelphia and Allegheny batch timing decides the pace. A call delay is normal, not an anomaly.

A county extends polling hours

Record the order, the county, the minutes extended, and how provisional ballots are handled.

A DOJ demand meets the California order

Name the court order and the state. The restraining order bars federal ballot-segregation demands (REPORTED).

The Alaska ruling lands, or does not

Branch on the text of Judge Gleason's order; silence means the fight spills into the count.

An Iran announcement lands during polls

Deal or force, the market and turnout framing changes. The Doha talks are the displacement risk (REPORTED).

The remote-access chip rule

BIS interim final rule 2026-23529, Addressing Remote Access to Advanced Semiconductors, scheduled to publish Thursday and take effect on publication. A license is required for exports to overseas data centers serving D:5 or Macau-headquartered firms, with a new license requirement for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil. US-headquartered companies are exempt with reporting; 60-day comments; the AI Diffusion Rule is rescinded.

Public inspection listing FACT. Rule details REPORTED (Pillsbury, Ashurst). Publication not confirmed on federalregister.gov this run (WATCH).

What we knew, what we learned today, what we are watching next

Each line picks up where the Nov 17 exhibit (The Assay Office) left off. The Nov 18 exhibit is not yet in the series repo; the Nov 18 board is quoted directly where it matters.

The summit line

  1. What we knew

    Nov 17 exhibit: Thailand called what it signed in Manila a trade statement, not a deal; the trilateral was set for Thursday in Shenzhen.

  2. What we learned today

    It happened: bilaterals, then the trilateral, then a four-point declaration released first by Chinese state media (REPORTED). No joint press conference. The desk logs a pattern: three days, three headlines without US text (the Thai statement, the Philippine deal, the Shenzhen declaration).

  3. What we are watching next

    White House text and any wording drift against the Chinese version; Kyiv, EU and NATO on the ICC clause. Checkpoint Fri Nov 20 (WATCH).

The Gulf line

  1. What we knew

    Nov 17 exhibit: one hull had a name and six claimed tankers did not. The Nov 18 board added the abandoned Tara Gas and a reported strike on a US radar at Isa Air Base.

  2. What we learned today

    Qeshm, struck Tuesday per an unnamed US official (REPORTED). CENTCOM was on record for Tuesday's Bahrain intercepts after all, which corrects the desk's earlier no-text note: absence on search is not silence. Kuwait drawn in; ADNOC vessel hit with 1 missing; a tanker hit off Khasab. The Morning Lock adds damage to the PIF Tower in Riyadh (REPORTED).

  3. What we are watching next

    On-record Qeshm text; Saudi attribution for Riyadh; ADNOC hull name. Checkpoint CENTCOM and UKMTO, Fri Nov 20 morning ET (WATCH).

The AI line

  1. What we knew

    Nov 17 exhibit: Nvidia's first report without Jensen Huang at 80. The Nov 18 board scheduled Nvidia's reaction, the FOMC minutes and Anthropic's pricing on one day.

  2. What we learned today

    Nvidia reported $115.8B and guided $130B with zero China data center (REPORTED). Anthropic priced at $83 and traded as high as $115.10 while the Dow fell 2 percent intraday (REPORTED). The chip rule was scheduled to take effect; Taipei fell 4.4 percent (single source).

  3. What we are watching next

    Closes; the Federal Register citation; ASEAN and Brazil responses; lockup terms. Checkpoint Evening Close, Thu Nov 19 (WATCH).

The Medicare line

  1. What we knew

    The Nov 18 board scored the CMS CRUSH rule at 60 while it sat on public inspection.

  2. What we learned today

    Insurers fell 10 to 13 percent Wednesday, so the desk raised the row to 70 and filed a calibration note: the board under-scored it. The rule was scheduled to print Thursday, and the collision map buries it for a second day.

  3. What we are watching next

    Federal Register citation and docket ID; comment letters; enrollment-verification language. Checkpoint Fri Nov 20 (WATCH).

The count line

    Nov 17 exhibit: Alaska at Peltola 49.2 percent, ranked choice tabulation due Wednesday.

  2. What we learned today

    AP projected Peltola on Tue Nov 17 with 50.1 percent of first choices (REPORTED, FairVote). The desk pruned its Nov 2022 flip precedent: the race is projected.

  3. What we are watching next

    The official RCV report and certification Nov 25 (WATCH).

The nominee line

  1. What we knew

    The Nov 18 board flagged an FDA commissioner vote at 9:45 AM as an Attention Gap candidate.

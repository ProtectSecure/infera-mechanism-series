The brokers become the gatekeepers

      
The same brokers buying your data also decide your housing, lending, insurance, and employment outcomes.

      
Acxiom, Experian, LexisNexis, CoreLogic. In this money flow they look like a middle tier — buying signal from platforms and reselling it to advertisers. In the next episode they reappear as the scoring layer that ranks you for the apartment, prices the mortgage, sets the insurance premium, and decides whether the applicant tracking system shows your résumé to a human.

08RECEIPTS

    

      
Every claim above
has a paper trail.
Here are six of them.

    

  


  


    

      

        
RCPT · 01
T1 · CORPORATE

        
Acxiom holds 1,500+ data points on 2.5 billion consumers.

        
Acxiom — the data broker quietly underpinning much of US ad targeting — publishes its own scale. The number is not a leak; it's a sales pitch. Your composite isn't theoretical. It already exists.

        
"More than 11,000 attributes across 2.5 billion consumers, with up to 1,500 attributes per person."

      

      
SOURCE · acxiom.com / customer-data · corporate fact sheet

    


    

      

        
RCPT · 02
T1 · FEDERAL

        
Facebook paid the FTC $5 billion for selling 87 million profiles.

        
The 2019 settlement is the largest privacy penalty in US history — entered after Facebook handed Cambridge Analytica access to 87M profiles that became political-microtargeting inventory. The vendor cycle exists because it works at scale.

        
"The $5 billion penalty against Facebook is the largest ever imposed on any company for violating consumers' privacy."

      

      
SOURCE · ftc.gov · 2019 press release

    


    

      

        
RCPT · 03
T2 · RESEARCH

        
TikTok's in-app browser silently injects a keystroke tracker.

        
Security researcher Felix Krause discovered that TikTok's in-app browser injects JavaScript on every external link tap — observing form inputs and taps even on sites unrelated to TikTok. The signal capture extends past the app boundary.

        
"TikTok iOS subscribes to every keystroke (text inputs) happening on third-party websites rendered inside the TikTok app."

      

      
SOURCE · krausefx.com · 2022 disclosure

    


    

      

        
RCPT · 04
T1 · REGULATORY

        
Dominion forecasts data centers will drive ~70% of Virginia load growth.

        
In its 2024 Integrated Resource Plan filed with the State Corporation Commission, Dominion Energy projects data-center demand as the dominant component of its forecasted load growth — to be built and recovered, in significant part, from residential ratepayers. Your bill is underwriting somebody else's compute.

        
"Data centers represent the largest single source of load growth, comprising the majority of incremental demand through 2039."

      

      
SOURCE · Dominion Energy · 2024 VA IRP filing · case PUR-2024-00050

    


    

      

        
RCPT · 05
T1 · FEDERAL

        
Spokeo paid the FTC $800K for selling profiles to employers.

        
The 2012 settlement found that Spokeo had assembled detailed personal profiles from online sources — including Social Security inferences — and sold them as employment screening reports without complying with the Fair Credit Reporting Act. People's profiles already affect hiring decisions in ways they cannot see or contest.

        
"Spokeo marketed its profiles on a subscription basis to human resources professionals as an employment screening tool."

      

      
SOURCE · ftc.gov · 2012 press release

    


    

      

        
RCPT · 06
T2 · INVESTIGATIVE

        
Meta's pixel tracked patient data on 33 of 100 top hospital sites.

        
A 2022 investigation by The Markup found Meta's tracking pixel collected sensitive patient data — including doctor names, appointment details, and condition queries — on a third of top US hospital websites and sent it back to Facebook for ad targeting. Sensitive context is not a barrier to broker capture.

        
"Facebook is collecting sensitive medical information about people, including health conditions and prescriptions, through tracking code on hundreds of hospital websites."

      

      
SOURCE · themarkup.org · Pixel Hunt 2022

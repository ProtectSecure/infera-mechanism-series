08 RESTRAINT · THE GOOD-FAITH READ · SOURCES

WHAT THIS BRIEF
DOES NOT CLAIM.

  • Not "Google is buying names." Consumer profiles and loyalty data are excluded; the story is referential integrity, scope, and whether the strip holds.
  • The raw-vs-sold perimeter is stated precisely, every time: third parties enter the pipeline; the promise is that they don't exit it.
  • The $12.5M late bid stays UNCONFIRMED until the docket says otherwise.
  • The strongest good-faith read gets its card: the estate chose the bid with the independent third-party deidentifier — governance structure was the tiebreaker — and the buyer states it will receive no personal information. If Branch A holds and the strip holds, we will say that too.
  • Red stays reserved. Nothing here has earned it.

00 ATLAS NODE · SEASON II SPINE THE DERIVATIVE PERSON

DERIVATIVE DATA

Definition · information manufactured from records rather than contained in them — a score, a portrait, a memory, a forecast, a deidentified-but-linked corpus. The person supplies the life. An institution manufactures the derivative. The derivative is the thing that acts.

I
DERIVATIVES OUTRANK THEIR SOURCES

The score matters more than the payments. The portrait feels truer than the chats. The forecast borrows more than the bookings earned. Institutions prefer the derivative — it's cheaper to read.

II
DERIVATIVES TRAVEL WHERE FACTS CAN'T

A payment history stays at one bank; the score walks into every lender, landlord, insurer, and carrier in the country. Portability without consent is the derivative's physics.

III
DELETING THE SOURCE DOESN'T DELETE THE DERIVATIVE

The chat is erased; the memory persists. The memory is erased; what training absorbed persists. The company dies; its corpus goes to auction. Unlearning is immature. Sold corpora don't come home.

We regulated the first derivative person in 1970. Then we forgot we'd done it.

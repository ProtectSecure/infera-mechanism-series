Room 2 · What the whole life shows

Protection arrives by the door you walk through.

The patternAcross one life the law shows up in a few places: when a thief takes your money, when a college takes federal aid, when a hospital treats you. Everywhere else, the file is opened under rules the institution wrote. No single law follows the person. INFERENCEfrom the tagged entries above

The other side

  • Most of these files exist to serve the person: a school must know its students, a hospital its patients, a bank its customers.
  • Some institutions build protections no law requires; banks on Zelle began refunding some impostor scams in 2023. REPORTED
  • A single law that followed the person would itself be a very large file about them.

What this remix cannot show

  • That any real person has all of these files, or that any are joined together. WATCH
  • State laws, which add protection in some places. See the state lens in The Loom.
  • A dollar value for the life's data. We did not find an honest one.

F04 · Pub 11/12 · gravity 66 · Likely coverage · FACT · LAWMAKING_RULES

Federal Register final rule: FCC National Television Multiple Ownership Rule

Who. FCC, broadcast station groups

Infera read. Publication starts the clock on effective date and court challenges; read the cap figure and UHF discount treatment before claiming direction (scheduled, withdrawable).

Update Wed Nov 11. The FCC is repealing the 39% national ownership cap outright, with no replacement number (Report and Order FCC 26-53, adopted 2 to 1 on Aug 6, released Oct 1; FACT). Deals that would have crossed 39% now get case-by-case public interest review. The order takes effect 60 days after Federal Register publication, so about Jan 11, 2027 if Thursday holds (INFERRED). The UHF discount sits in the repealed rule and most likely goes with it; the final text was not read (WATCH). A state cable associations stay petition was filed Oct 2 (REPORTED). Checkpoint: Thu Nov 12 publication, then court petitions.

Analog. 2003 FCC 45% cap backlash that led Congress to set 39%; 2017 UHF discount restoration

Next: Effective date; petitions for review; pending station-group mergers. Checkpoint: Thu Nov 12, Federal Register publication. Dated Tue Nov 10 board. Source (T1)

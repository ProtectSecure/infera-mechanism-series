THE DETAIL THAT TELLS YOU WHERE THIS GOES

Read the target list like a sentence. Iran's loudest threat is closing the strait. The way a navy closes a strait is mines. The thing the US says it just destroyed, more than anything else, is naval mine storage. The strike was not retaliation for Friday's drones, it was an attempt to repossess Iran's ability to make good on the closure threat before it could be used.

That single fact moves this from "another exchange" to a contest over whether the threat itself stays credible. Both de-escalation and the ugliest branch run directly through it.

HALL II

The cliff is slower than advertised, and that changes the politics

CASE FILE · §702, THE ADVANCE
NEW ANALYSIS
No.04 told you the law expires June 12. Here is what it could not tell you. A lapse does not switch the lights off at midnight, because existing court approved certifications stay valid until their own expiration dates, months from now. Collection continues under them. What stops is the issuing of anything new. So the real cliff is not June 12, it is a slow ratchet: every month of lapse burns off more of the program with no way to replace it, which means the leverage compounds in favor of whoever can wait. And the overnight escalation just flipped the table. A live shooting exchange is the strongest argument the renewal side has been handed all year, surge collection during a crisis, and simultaneously the strongest argument the holdouts have, crisis is exactly when an untrusted hand on the instrument matters most. Watch one more clock nobody has connected yet: the strikes themselves trigger a War Powers notification to Congress within 48 hours, due roughly Sunday, landing on the same floor, the same week, as the §702 fight.

WHY THIS REACHES YOU

Two of the most consequential questions in American life, who may read communications without a warrant and who decides when the country trades fire abroad, just landed in the same room in the same week, each one now a bargaining chip in the other. That collision is brand new. Nobody wrote about it this morning.

The rule of the loom

What a pairing can and cannot show.

What it can do

  • Put two sourced mechanisms side by side so the shared shape is visible.
  • Show a real fork: the same kind of event handled under two different rules.
  • Turn a gap into a question worth reporting.

What it cannot do

  • Prove that one thing caused another. A shared thread is a resemblance. INFERENCE
  • Upgrade a claim. A fact tagged REPORTED at home is REPORTED here.
  • Fill silence. Where two pieces have no documented link, the loom says so.

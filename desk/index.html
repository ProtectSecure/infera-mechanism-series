The newsroom wire feed.

Six scheduled products that capture the public schedule, the deltas, and the gaps. The substrate the Briefs are built on. Not editorial product; operational infrastructure.

The Desk is where the operational layer lives. Six scheduled tasks run on a fixed cadence — 4 PM Tomorrow Board, 6:30 AM Morning Lock, 8:45 AM Federal Register PI scan, 6:30 PM Evening Close, 11:30 PM Overnight Desk delta, and the Monday 9 AM Weekly Accountability Sweep. Each one captures something specific from the public schedule and writes it to disk.

This is what feeds the Briefs. It is not the Brief. The Brief is the editorial product extracted from this raw stream. The Desk shows the stream itself.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

The Desk is not a Brief. The Brief synthesizes; the Desk captures. A typical Tomorrow Board contains 40-80 line items across federal, state, and county layers, with gravity scores and cross-state convergence flags. A Brief picks one of those items and turns it into a story. Both are public-source, but only the Brief has a voice.

SCHEDULED PRODUCTS6 LIVE

01
Tomorrow Board
The next-day Open Record Order. Pulls federal layer (Senate, House, agencies, SCOTUS, BLS/BEA/Census), NC + PA state layers (legislatures, courts, PUCs, auditors), and the L2 county layer (Legistar, CivicPlus, OpenCities, Sitefinity, Cadence Projection). Tags each item with civic dimension and gravity score. Flags cross-state convergence.
4:04 PM ET · MON-FRI LIVE
02
Morning Lock
Confirms the Tomorrow Board before the 8:30 AM ET economic-release window. Checks for overnight cancellations, time changes, or new postings. Locks the day's record order or flags it for revision.
6:38 AM ET · MON-FRI LIVE
03
Federal Register PI Scan
Pulls the Federal Register's Public Inspection list — rules and proposed rules posted that morning that take effect later. Tags by agency, by mechanism, by likely downstream impact. The day's governance preview before it's law.
8:54 AM ET · MON-FRI LIVE
04
Evening Close
Compares planned versus actual. What was on the Tomorrow Board that did happen, what was on the Tomorrow Board that did not, what happened that was not on the Tomorrow Board. The attention-gap output names the high-gravity items that received no media coverage that day.
6:38 PM ET · MON-FRI LIVE
05
Overnight Desk
Captures everything that changed since the Tomorrow Board fired. New filings, withdrawn rules, shifted agendas, court orders, late-evening press releases. The morning's delta is built here.
11:32 PM ET · DAILY LIVE
06
Weekly Accountability Sweep
Pulls the GovInfo Congressionally Mandated Reports collection, GAO open recommendations, Oversight.gov, and OIG semiannual reports. Builds the week's list of overdue reports and unmet recommendations. The slow-clock layer.
9:07 AM ET · MONDAYS LIVE

OUTPUTS · THREE LAYERSJOURNAL + CALENDAR + ARCHIVE

The Desk publishes through three distinct surfaces. The Journal is the reader-facing zine that renders the overnight cycle as a designed artifact. The Sealed Calendar is the forward watchlist of court-sealed material scheduled to become public. The Archive is the raw dated artifacts the scheduled tasks write to disk. All three are public-source.

The Infera Desk Journal

The living mixed-media board. The overnight desk cycle rendered as a zine: featured case files, background-radiation thread strip, deadline ledger, confidence labels per claim. Updated each cycle. The only Desk surface with a voice.

THE SEALED CALENDAR · FORWARD DISCLOSURE WATCHLIST

The PA Sealed Calendar

Every protective-order sunset, statutory unsealing date, gag-order expiration, and phased settlement disclosure across EDPA, WDPA, the Third Circuit, and PA bankruptcy courts in the next 90 days. THE ARCHIVE · DATED ARTIFACTS

Raw outputs land in dated artifacts: tomorrow-board-YYYY-MM-DD.{csv,md}, overnight-delta-YYYY-MM-DD.md, evening-close-YYYY-MM-DD.md, weekly-accountability-sweep-YYYY-MM-DD.md. The archive is permanent. Most artifacts do not get promoted to the Journal; the archive is the point.

The secondary signal: when a scheduled unsealing fails to occur.

When a date passes and an expected document does not appear, that is itself a story. The Sealed Calendar tracks each entry's failure-to-unseal alert date and surfaces overdue items here. Patterns of which seals get extended, by which judges, in which case types are themselves a longitudinal story across enough events. Apply the blind-spot auditor before treating any single non-unsealing as news; a seal not lifting on schedule may be administrative, not significant.

No confirmed failures to unseal, June 7 to October 22. Three June projections (the Q2 opioid monitor report, the Crozer sale-status exhibits and the August PUC filings) produced no disclosure, but each rested on a premise that could not be verified, so under the blind-spot discipline they are logged above as CHANGED, not as failures.

Methodology · how to read this page honestly

What this page is. A forward calendar of disclosure events in PA federal courts, powered by the infera-sealed-document-watcher skill. The premise is that most seals have a structure (duration, sunset clause, triggering event, review cycle) and that the structure is usually public even when the sealed content is not. Mining the structure produces a calendar.

What it is not. This page does not display sealed content. It displays the public schedule on which sealed content is expected to become available. It does not solicit, request, or process sealed material. Entries are scheduled-future events, not current disclosures.

Failure-to-unseal handling. A scheduled unsealing that does not occur on its expected date is escalated to the failure-to-unseal section after the alert date passes and the docket shows no unsealing activity. Per the skill's own discipline, the blind-spot auditor is applied before any non-unsealing is treated as news; the most common explanation is administrative delay, not concealment.

Out-of-scope categories. Certain seal categories (grand jury materials under FRCrP 6(e), juvenile records, certain national-security materials) are tracked at the metadata level only. The Sealed Calendar observes their statutory schedule but does not process the underlying material when unsealing occurs. See infera-sealed-document-watcher/RULES-AND-METHODOLOGY.md Section III.

03 · THE METHOD

Gravity ranks it. Confidence qualifies it.

Coverage gravity blends power proximity, institutional venue, camera availability, conflict level, novelty, recurrence, and same-time collision — with power proximity capped so no event inflates on proximity to the president alone. Every point traces to a structural feature of the event. Confidence is stamped separately, because how loud a story is and how sure we are are two different questions.

FACT

On the public surface now — a scheduled release, a filed document, a recorded vote count. Verifiable against a primary source.

REPORTED

Carried by sourcing we trust but have not independently confirmed, or attributed to a single or adversarial source. Treated as a signal, not a settled fact.

WATCH

Scheduled but withdrawable, projected from a known cadence, or running on a cached snapshot. Flagged, re-polled, never asserted as done.

Tiers run T1 (official primary source) through T4 (attention-optimized). Urgency without panic: every alert carries a date, a confidence label, and a next checkpoint.

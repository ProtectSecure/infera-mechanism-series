MOMENT 05 / 07 · DISCIPLINE
THE THREE REFUSALS · WHAT THIS INSTALLATION DOES NOT CLAIM

No conspiracy.
No determinism.
No despair.

i.
No conspiracy.
The machine is not coordinated. Naming an emergent pattern is not naming a plot. The composite is assembled by competition, not collusion.
ii.
No determinism.
Not every household is sorted the same way. Not every chapter applies equally. Every modeled aggregate in this installation is labeled as modeled.
iii.
No despair.
EP 12 The Governance Layer names the political instruments that change this. Outcomes are produced, not given.

DEFENDER 1 · FAccT

"The mechanisms' opacity is contingent, not necessary. EU AI Act. Colorado AI Act. NYC AEDT. The trajectory is toward visibility, not away from it."

DEFENDER 2 · INDUSTRY

"No single decision intends discrimination. FCRA, FHA, Title VII, HIPAA, ECOA already govern these outcomes. The manual baseline produced worse disparities."

DEFENDER 3 · SOCIOLOGICAL SKEPTIC

"Cumulative disadvantage has explanatory range but empirical fragility. The mechanism may be real; magnitude attribution is the open question."

⟶ CURATOR'S NOTE · ON THE FORM

This episode is built as an installation rather than an article because cumulative disadvantage is a felt phenomenon, not a read one. The reader spends 90 seconds becoming the engine — not because the engine is gameable, but because the felt knowledge of having sorted strangers is the only honest preparation for meeting the strangers who were sorted. The five families are real cases from Season I; the breadcrumbs under each card are the receipt. The conceptual approach is documented in the EP 08 brief, the 53-claim corpus, the anchor-families document, the Class Atlas spec, and the counter-read outreach plan in _drafts/.

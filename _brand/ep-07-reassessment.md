> **Companion to `_brand/season-architecture.md`** · Strategic editorial reassessment of the EP 07 brief after Season I expanded to 10 episodes — specifically after S1·06 The Vogtle Bill, S1·07 The Data Center Bill, and S1·09 The Capital Stack landed with specific reported numbers, named actors, and a documented reform agenda.

## The premise

The original EP 07 brief was drafted in May 2026, when Season I covered five field stories (Trickle, Eviction Funnel, Closed Hospital, Coast, Provisional Ballot) and the financial-substrate sub-arc didn't yet exist on the homepage. The brief had to introduce the rate-payer mechanism, the HB 1192 tax architecture, the load forecast, the cost split, the reform agenda — because no other piece in the publication had named those things.

That premise no longer holds. With S1·07 The Data Center Bill now live and reported, the rate-payer mechanism story has been told in detail. With S1·06 The Vogtle Bill specifying the $35B kitchen-table-to-dividend flow and the December 2023 PSC vote, the cost-socialization template is named. With S1·09 The Capital Stack integrating Vogtle + data centers + donations as one stack, the architectural rhyme between financial and physical substrate is now available.

**EP 07's job has changed.** It no longer needs to introduce. It needs to use, scale, and converge.

## The four framing changes

### 1 · Compression point locked

**Before:** *"The cloud is a county in northern Virginia, an electric substation in Phoenix, a water permit in Quincy, and a tax abatement most residents have never read."*

**After:** ***"You have seen the bill. Now see the building. Then see the building in your state."***

This makes the Season I → EP 07 handoff explicit ("you have seen the bill" reads as a direct callback to S1·07 The Data Center Bill) and lands the national escalation in three beats. A reader who hasn't read S1·07 still parses the line as a complete claim; a reader who has read it feels the architecture click into place. The new line also opens the question that the national lever map interactive will answer: *what about my state?*

### 2 · Chassis restructured from 3 acts to 4

The original PIPELINE act was overloaded — it had to cover what's inside the building AND who pays AND what the cost stacking looks like. With S1·07 carrying the WHO-PAYS work, the body restructures into four clean acts:

| Act | What it does | Why it's distinct from S1·07 |
|---|---|---|
| **I · THE ANATOMY** | Cinematic walk through what's inside a hyperscale facility — server rows, UPS banks, switchgear, chillers, liquid-cooling lines for AI workloads, fiber distribution, generator yards. The building the reader has never been allowed inside. | S1·07 didn't cover the building's interior. The anatomy is genuinely new editorial work. |
| **II · THE OWNERSHIP** | Four hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Meta) plus colocation REIT layer (Equinix, Digital Realty, CyrusOne, Iron Mountain) plus GovCloud / Azure Government / Google Public Sector. The ownership concentration mapped at the scale of buildings, not just at the scale of platforms. | S1·07 named the hyperscalers as customers; EP 07 maps them as owners and operators with concentration math. |
| **III · THE PATTERN** | The Georgia mechanism is reproducing in Loudoun County VA, Maricopa County AZ, central Ohio, Pacific Northwest. Same tax-holiday architecture (each state's variant of HB 1192). Same rate-allocation socialization. Same three-piece reform agenda. Same blocker pattern. | S1·07 is Georgia-specific. EP 07 nationalizes the case by treating Georgia as the documented proof and showing the structural template. |
| **IV · THE CONVERGENCE** | The same hyperscale buildings host both the commercial composite (EP 02 Personhood Inc., EP 03 Trust Market) AND the civic composite (EP 06 Composite State). AWS GovCloud and AWS commercial share campuses. The data-layer fragmentation that EP 06 named as a structural defense **does not exist at the physical layer**. | Genuinely new — connects EP 06 → EP 07 → EP 08 with a load-bearing thesis. |

This is sharper, more honest about what EP 07 adds, and avoids the awkward retelling-Season-I problem.

### 3 · Interactive replaced with a National Lever Map

**Before:** Four-stage interactive ending with "WHAT IT COSTS YOU" — which estimated the per-state residential contribution to data-center grid buildout. But that's now S1·07's job for Georgia (and only Georgia).

**After:** A **National Lever Map** modeled on the planned EP 12 Governance Layer interactive. The reader selects their state. The map surfaces:

- Data-center buildout to date (MW installed + announced)
- Estimated annual residential contribution to grid upgrades supporting that load
- Whether the state has a **hyperscale tariff** authorized (yes/no/proposed)
- Whether the state has a **tax-exemption sunset** (yes/no/proposed)
- Whether **per-facility disclosure** is required (yes/no/proposed)
- Who the PSC commissioners are, how they're elected, and when their next election is
- For each "no" the reader sees, the closest peer state that has it (so reform feels reachable, not theoretical)

This is the reader's first comparative-jurisdiction tool in the publication. It converts the Georgia reporting into something every reader in every host state can use. It also reads in conversation with the EP 12 Governance Layer Lever Map — two interactives, two scales, same logic.

### 4 · Capital Stack architecture rhyme made explicit

S1·09 The Capital Stack named the three-instrument financial substrate. EP 07 should explicitly name itself as the **physical mirror** of that substrate:

> *Capital Stack mapped how the dollar moves. The Physical Plant maps where the dollar stops moving and starts taking up space. The hyperscalers that built the financial stack also built the physical stack. The PSC that approves the rate case also approves the substation siting. The reform agenda that dies in committee is the same reform agenda in both places.*

This rhyme — financial substrate ↔ physical substrate — is itself a load-bearing observation about how political-economic architecture and physical architecture coevolve. The publication only earns it because Season I documented Capital Stack so specifically. EP 07 takes the next step.

## Two new editorial moves Season I makes possible

### A · The reform-agenda-travels moment

S1·07 documented the three-piece reform agenda (hyperscale tariff / HB 1192 sunset / per-facility disclosure) and named why it dies in committee in Georgia. EP 07 can now do something stronger: show that the **same three-piece reform agenda is being introduced in the legislatures of every major data-center state** — Virginia, Arizona, Ohio, Oregon, Texas — **and dying in the same way**, for structurally similar reasons (PSC commissioner composition, low-turnout elections, hyperscaler-adjacent donor architecture).

This is not parallel storytelling. It's *one architecture, replicated*. The opposition isn't local. The blocker isn't local. The reform pattern isn't local. The piece that earns this claim is EP 07 — and only because S1·07 made the Georgia case so defensible first.

This is also the strongest possible setup for EP 12 The Governance Layer's Lever Map: by EP 12, the reader already knows what reform looks like and why it stalls. EP 12 hands them the specific instruments and named contacts to push it forward in their own state.

### B · The reactor-cycle preview

S1·06 The Vogtle Bill closed with a key observation: the next reactor cycle is already initiating, and hyperscaler nuclear PPAs are signing. EP 07 can pick this up explicitly:

> The AI compute load is the demand that justifies the next round of Vogtle-pattern reactor construction. The reader sees the financial loop close: data centers triggered the load forecast; the load forecast justifies new generation; new generation will become the next $35B kitchen-table-to-dividend story; **and the data centers will be the largest single customer of that generation while paying the smallest share of its cost.**

The publication only earns this claim because Season I documented Vogtle so specifically. It's a forward-looking compounding-cost prediction backed by the precise mechanism Season I established. It reads like investigative journalism (specific, grounded, falsifiable) rather than speculation (which the original EP 07 brief had to lean on more heavily without Season I's foundation).

This preview also sets up the EP 08 Class Engine bowtie cleanly: the cost compounding the reader watches in EP 07 is the same cost compounding EP 08 will name as the engine that produces the families Season I has documented.

## What stays the same

The reassessment is additive. The following elements from the original EP 07 brief should carry forward unchanged:

- **Six-act chassis** (HERO → PRESSURE → PIPELINE → OUTCOME → COMPETING → DISCIPLINE → CLOSER) at the macro level; the four-act PIPELINE restructure happens inside that
- **Substation Field visual grammar** — stylized aerial of a representative campus with breathing-pulse electrical load and counter-pulse heat plumes
- **Counter-read defenders** — economic development / federalism / sectoral approach / market correction; all four still apply
- **DISCIPLINE refusals** — no conspiracy / no dystopia / no mind-control framing; all three still apply
- **EP 08 handoff** — *"the cost is documented; the convergence is in EP 08; the bowtie is where you'll see what these compounding costs sort the Season I families into"*

## Editorial position after the reassessment

EP 07 The Physical Plant becomes a different kind of piece than originally planned. It's no longer an introduction to a cost mechanism — it's the **nationalization** of a cost mechanism already documented in Georgia, plus the **physical convergence claim** that sets up EP 08's bowtie. It does anatomy + ownership + national pattern + commercial/civic convergence — four distinct editorial jobs, none of them duplicating Season I.

The reader who finishes EP 07 should leave with:

1. A working mental model of what's inside one of these buildings
2. A working mental model of who owns them and how concentration manifests
3. The conviction that the Georgia pattern (S1·06 + S1·07) is the national pattern
4. The realization that the legal-data fragmentation EP 06 named as a structural defense does not exist at the physical layer
5. A specific reform agenda they can press in their own state, with a comparative tool that shows them what other states have already done
6. The setup for EP 08 — the cost compounding they just watched is the engine producing outcomes already documented in Season I

The publication's editorial coherence increased materially. The brief should be rewritten to match.

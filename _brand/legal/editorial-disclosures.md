## § METHODOLOGY

The Mechanism Series follows a four-part method on every claim.

**Mechanism, not verdict.** We do not write conclusions and then look for evidence. We pick an observable system, reverse-engineer the upstream pressure → legal scaffolding → downstream outcome, and let the reader switch interpretive lenses on the same set of facts. A claim that cannot be traced through a lawful, documented mechanism does not get published.

**Source tiering.** Every external source is tagged at one of four reliability tiers (T1 primary official document · T2 named secondary reporting · T3 published research · T4 contextual commentary). Tier appears in the citation footer of every paragraph.

**Confidence stamping.** Every load-bearing claim carries one of four stamps: **Strong** (multi-source, primary-document supported), **Plausible** (single-source or inference from primary data), **Correlated** (statistically observable without proven causation), **Competing** (named disagreement we surface alongside our reading). Stamps are visible to the reader, not buried in methods.

**Discipline section.** Every episode ends with a section that names what we did NOT prove, what alternative readings deserve hearing, and what data we wished we had. The section is a feature, not an apology. If we can't articulate the limits of our own claim, we have not understood it well enough to publish.

We invented none of this. It's adapted from OCCRP, ICIJ, IRE, the Brennan Center research desk, and Drew Sullivan's "reverse engineering power." We name our intellectual lineage publicly.

## § SOURCES POLICY

We link to the most specific publicly accessible page we can find for every cited claim. When a primary filing requires navigation, login, or a records request from the linked landing page, we name the specific filing in the source label. If a link does not resolve directly to the exact document, this is a navigation limit, not a fabrication.

We re-verify every cited URL on every site deploy. Broken or moved links are repaired within 48 hours of discovery. Our trust-audit log is published at infera.studio/trust (forthcoming).

We do not pay for sources. We do not grant anonymity to sources whose claims we cannot independently corroborate.

## § CORRECTIONS POLICY (summary)

See the corrections section in the footer-legal page. In brief: every correction gets acknowledged within 48 hours, reviewed within 5 business days, and either published with a dated note on the affected episode, or rebutted with reasoning.

## § CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

The Infera editorial desk discloses, on the relevant episode, any material conflict of interest. Material conflicts include:

- A staff editor's direct financial interest in a named subject
- A staff editor's familial or close personal relationship with a named decision-maker
- An underwriter's commercial relationship with a named subject

Staff editors do not own individual stocks in any company whose conduct could appear in our investigations. (Diversified index funds are not a conflict.)

If a conflict surfaces mid-investigation, the affected piece is reassigned or transparently flagged.

## § UNDERWRITING & FUNDING

Infera is supported by the reader newsletter, occasional research engagements, and a small number of named foundations underwriting specific Georgia field work. All underwriters are listed publicly on the index footer and on the specific underwritten episode.

No advertiser pays Infera. No platform deal exists. No political party, candidate, advocacy organization, lobbying firm, or government agency funds the Mechanism Series.

Underwriters do not see episodes prior to publication. Editorial decisions are independent of underwriting decisions and the people who make each are different humans.

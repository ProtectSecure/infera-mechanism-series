## § PRIVACY STATEMENT

Plain version: we collect almost nothing, and we don't share what little we collect.

**What we collect when you visit**
- Server logs from our host (Render.com) — IP, user-agent, page requested, timestamp. Retained 30 days for security and debugging. Not joined to any identity.
- *No* third-party tracking pixels. *No* Google Analytics. *No* Meta Pixel. *No* advertising cookies. The site uses zero third-party analytics by design.
- One first-party browser local-storage value for the persona system in Season II, which lets the publication remember which persona you picked so the cross-episode echoes work. Cleared on browser clear. Never transmitted.

**What we collect if you subscribe**
- Your email address, the date you subscribed, and the page you subscribed from.
- Subscriptions on the current site are submitted directly to infera@melophon.com via the reader's mail client. The list is kept manually; no third-party newsletter platform is in use.

**What we collect if you email us**
- Whatever you sent us, and our reply. Stored in Google Workspace (Gmail), governed by Google's privacy terms.

**What we never do**
- Sell, rent, or share your email with anyone, for any reason
- Build advertising audiences from your reading behavior
- Cross-reference visitors with broker profiles (this would be hypocritical given what we cover)
- Use tracking pixels in email or on the site

**Your rights**
- **Access**: write to infera@melophon.com and ask what we have on you. We respond within five days.
- **Deletion**: write to infera@melophon.com or click unsubscribe at the bottom of any newsletter.
- **Correction**: write to infera@melophon.com.
- **EU / UK readers**: you have the rights described in Articles 15–22 of the GDPR. We honor all of them.
- **California readers**: you have the rights described in CCPA §§ 1798.100–199. We honor all of them.

## § CORRECTIONS POLICY

Corrections are the most important email we get. Send them to infera@melophon.com with the specific claim, the specific episode, and the supporting evidence.

**We will**:
- Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
- Review the claim within 5 business days
- Publish a dated correction note on the affected episode if we agree
- Publish a dated *response* (with our reasoning) if we disagree
- Update the corresponding confidence stamp on the original claim

We log every correction in a single accessible record at series.melophon.com/corrections (forthcoming).

## § ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

The Mechanism Series is committed to WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.

**What we do**:
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere — animations drop to near-zero duration when readers request it
- Use semantic HTML throughout
- Maintain visible focus states on every interactive element
- Test contrast at AA minimum for all body text
- Provide a `Skim` reading mode on every Season I episode that strips supporting prose to the load-bearing claims and citations

**Where we fall short**:
- The Season I episodes use heavy canvas animation that can be slow on older devices. The reduced-motion media query disables it.
- Some interactive visualizations are not fully screen-reader-navigable yet. We are working on this.
- Mobile rendering is improving episode-by-episode; not all motion is fully stripped on small screens yet.

**Report issues** to infera@melophon.com with the page URL, your browser/device, and a description. We respond within five business days.

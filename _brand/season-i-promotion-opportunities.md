# Season I Promotion · Future Storytelling Opportunities

> **Companion to `_brand/season-architecture.md`** · Reassessment after promoting Settlement Shrinkage, Capital Stack, and Runoff Tells from canonical-but-unlinked status to full Season I tile status (June 2 2026). Captures the new connections, experiments, and editorial moves the promotion opens up.

---

## What changed

Three reported field investigations that were sitting on disk — substantial, sourced, ready — moved from "accessible only by direct URL" to **promoted tiles on the homepage Season I row**. Plus a re-editorialization pass that brought the six-degrees architecture inside their body prose, not just at their closer.

Season I went from a 7-episode publication to a **10-episode publication** without writing any new field reporting. The pieces were already there. The architecture they participate in was the missing layer.

---

## What this earns the publication

### 1. A genuinely 10-mechanism Season I

The "Seven mechanisms. One pattern." closer thesis becomes **"Ten mechanisms. One pattern."** That's not a marketing change — it's an editorial promotion that makes Season I substantially more comprehensive as a survey of structural pressure in Georgia. The new range:

| Pressure type | Episodes |
|---|---|
| Housing / civic disappearance | S1·01 Trickle · S1·02 Eviction Funnel · S1·03 Closed Hospital · S1·04 Coast Is Moving |
| Civic apparatus | S1·05 Provisional Ballot · S1·10 Runoff Tells |
| Financial substrate | S1·06 Vogtle Bill · S1·07 Data Center Load · S1·09 Capital Stack |
| Disbursement & accountability | S1·08 Settlement Shrinkage |

The financial-substrate cluster (S1·06–S1·07–S1·09) now reads as a **three-piece sub-arc** within Season I — Vogtle is the historical case, Data Center Load is the present-tense case, Capital Stack is the integrative case that names the architecture across both. That's a coherent narrative pocket worth surfacing.

### 2. Two new explicit bridges to Season II

The re-editorialization added in-body callbacks at decisive points:

- **Settlement Shrinkage → EP 03 Trust Market** via the "algorithmic disbursement tiering" mechanism. The disbursement-tier-by-non-statutory-categorization observation in S1·08 Act III now sits right next to a pink-rule callout linking to the scoring architecture that produces this kind of tiering at scale.
- **Runoff Tells → EP 04 Influence War** via the "targeting infrastructure surface" framing. The bridge block right before the lens console now names the runoff as the *surface trace* of an infrastructure documented in EP 04.
- **Capital Stack → EP 02 + EP 03 + EP 08** via the "financial mirror of the data stack" framing. The bridge block surfaces the architecture rhyme: rate-base recovery is to ratepayers what scoring securitization is to people.

These are not generic "see also" links. They are **mechanism-specific causal claims** that make the hidden spine legible from inside the Season I body — which is the strongest form of the six-degrees architecture.

### 3. Three new pink edges on the Systems Atlas

| Edge | Causal claim |
|---|---|
| S1·08 Settlement Shrinkage ← EP 03 Trust Market | Disbursement scoring is the same architecture as commercial trust scoring |
| S1·09 Capital Stack ← EP 03 + EP 08 | Financial securitization architecture is the model for the composite securitization in EP 08 |

The Systems Atlas now has **11 pink load-bearing edges** total. The bowtie at EP 08 The Class Engine pulls from more S1 anchors than before — every additional Season I node strengthens the convergence claim.

## New storytelling opportunities this opens

### A · A "Financial Substrate" sub-arc landing page

S1·06 + S1·07 + S1·09 now read as a coherent three-piece investigation. **Build a small landing/index page** that frames them as a sub-arc:

- *The Vogtle Bill* — the historical case (15 years, $35B, kitchen table to dividend)
- *The Data Center Load* — the present-tense case (8,200 MW, 76% ratepayer absorption)
- *The Capital Stack* — the integrative case (three instruments, one stack, reproducible)

URL slug candidate: `/the-financial-substrate.html` or `/financial-substrate-of-georgia.html`. Acts like a "season within the season." Editorial leverage: someone landing on Vogtle can be invited to read the whole sub-arc; someone landing on Capital Stack can be invited to read the prequel.

### B · Runoff Tells live-updating addendum

The piece is dated May 22 2026 — pre-runoff. **The June 16 runoff results are now in or imminent.** Editorial opportunity: a small dated "RUNOFF UPDATE" block at the top of the page after the runoff, summarizing what the data actually showed vs. what the piece predicted, with confidence-tagged updates. Becomes the publication's first **prediction-verification example** — a powerful demonstration of the source-confidence framework actually working in time.

### C · Settlement Shrinkage as a recurring instrument

Settlement Shrinkage is the shortest piece (~764 words) but has the most natural **recurring-update cadence** — every settlement disbursement cycle (twice yearly, by the looks of it) provides fresh data to track. Editorial opportunity: position S1·08 as the first piece in a **recurring annual settlement tracker** that updates each cycle. Becomes a discoverability anchor — readers searching for "Georgia opioid settlement spending" land here over time.

### D · The Capital Stack interactive

The piece already has a "stack" hero visualization. Editorial opportunity to extend: a **follow-a-dollar interactive** that lets the reader pick a Georgia ZIP code, see the three concurrent claims on their household — Vogtle line item, data center load increment, regulator-adjacent donor influence — and watch them aggregate. Mirrors the EP 07 Physical Plant interactive but for Georgia specifically. Reuses the architecture; localizes the numbers.

### E · EP 08 Class Engine's housing-economics chapter gets new anchors

EP 08 The Class Engine (drafting) already plans to trace housing outcomes back to specific Season I families. With S1·09 Capital Stack now promoted and explicit about how ratepayer burden compounds with wage stagnation (S1·04 The Trickle), the housing chapter of EP 08 can now name **two upstream pressure stacks** (data/scoring + utility/financial) that converge on the same household. The bowtie becomes denser and more defensible.

### F · Foreshadow possibilities for Season III

Settlement Shrinkage's "audit-trail decay" framing is a natural setup for Season III's **EP S3·03 The Custodian Gap** — the episode about who is currently managing the integration role. The disbursement-audit gap S1·08 documents at a tactical level is the same shape of gap S3·03 documents at a structural level. Worth weaving the connection when Season III enters DISCOVER.

The Runoff Tells' "diagnostic instrument" framing (using runoff as a way to read what the broader electorate decided to skip) is a natural setup for **EP S3·04 What You Can Actually Do** — the agentic episode. Showing readers that runoff participation is an *especially leveraged* civic act maps directly to the calibrated-actions thesis of S3·04.

The Capital Stack's "reproducible across asset classes" framing is a natural setup for **the eventual Season II EP 08 Class Engine bowtie reveal**, AND for the Season III worst-case EP S3·01 — the *administrative consolidation* concern. The financial substrate IS the administrative consolidation, just at a different layer.

### G · Discoverability experiments

Now that Season I has 10 tiles, the homepage has more density. Worth testing:
- **A "start here" route** that guides a new reader through 3 specific episodes that establish the publication's frame fastest (proposed: S1·01 Trickle → S1·04 Coast Is Moving → S1·06 Vogtle Bill — each one shows a different kind of pressure)
- **A "by topic" filter** that lets the reader browse housing pieces, civic pieces, financial pieces — useful once Season I exceeds about 7 tiles
- **A Season I index page** at `/season-i.html` that gives the publication's first season its own permanent reference URL distinct from the homepage scroll

## What this re-edit does NOT solve (and what to track for the next pass)

1. **Where-you-are anchors are now on 3 Season I pages** (the promoted three) but **not the other 7**. For full coherence, the anchor pattern should extend to all 10 Season I episodes. Next pass: write a script that adds the same anchor template to Eviction Funnel, Closed Hospital, Coast, Trickle, Provisional Ballot, Vogtle, Data Center Load. Same approach as the Editorial Desk insert pass — standardize, batch.

2. **In-body callbacks** were added at one decisive point each in the three new tiles. They could be added at MORE points within each piece for even denser cross-referencing. But that risks tipping into "see also spam" the Reader-Experience Principle warns against. Hold for now; revisit if reader behavior shows the existing callbacks are being followed at high rates.

3. **The three new pages don't yet have breadcrumb-back blocks** (the Season II-style "the families this mechanism was secretly about" footer block). Season I pieces describe consequences, not mechanisms, so the breadcrumb-back pattern doesn't apply cleanly. The Editorial Desk insert + the where-you-are anchor + the in-body callbacks together cover the architecture without needing a fourth retention device.

4. **The atlas now has 11 pink edges** — getting visually dense. At 15+ edges the bowtie diagram will start to feel cluttered. Worth a future polish task: add an interactive "filter by movement" control so the reader can show only consequence edges, only extension edges, etc. Holds the architecture legible as the publication grows.

---

## Bottom line

The three pages were always part of the canon. Promoting them to the homepage and re-editorializing them with in-body callbacks does three things at once:

1. **Strengthens Season I as a comprehensive survey** — 10 mechanisms instead of 7, with a coherent financial-substrate sub-arc emerging.
2. **Strengthens the six-degrees architecture** — three more in-body callbacks to Season II mechanisms, three new pink edges on the Systems Atlas.
3. **Strengthens EP 08 The Class Engine** before it's even written — the bowtie now pulls from more Season I anchors, so the load-bearing convergence claim becomes harder to argue against.

Net effect: the publication's editorial coherence increased materially without any new field reporting. The architecture was already capable; the inventory just needed to be promoted into it.

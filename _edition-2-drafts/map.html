How to read the network.

This is not a finished argument — it is a navigable hypothesis space. Stories sit in the middle, surrounded by the upstream pressures they trace and the downstream consequences they document. An edge between two nodes is a claim that one influences the other — claims we mark with the same confidence framework the stories use. Hovering or clicking any node reveals what it's connected to, with confidence levels per edge.

This is the version of Infera the critique asked for: a systems-intelligence interface, not a publication. The stories tell single threads of the same fabric. The Map shows the fabric. Most political coverage shows neither the threads nor the fabric — it shows the headline event and asks you to fill in the rest.

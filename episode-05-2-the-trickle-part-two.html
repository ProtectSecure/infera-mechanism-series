WHAT THIS EPISODE
DOES NOT CLAIM.

  • No crash call. No race to a verdict the data hasn't rendered.
  • No individual-outcome attribution — no household's foreclosure is "because of" the layoff without its own record.
  • The union's −4,300 is an estimate and is labeled one, everywhere it appears.
  • Estate and relocation listings are controls, not evidence.
  • The absorption branch publishes with equal prominence. That is the deal.
  • Red stays reserved until the record itself turns adverse.

Act IV · The Log Carries Forward

What we knew, what we learned, what we watch.

Yesterday's exhibit is the Stone of June 12, and there is a gap behind this one: no boards exist for the summer and fall, so the thread below is stitched from Field Notes 12 to 14 and the overnight delta rather than from a daily chain.

What we knew
FISA 702 lapse

June 12 was set as the first lapse in program history. By FN13 it had run since June 13, with the House extension failed 198-218 and FISC certifications running to about March 2027 (FACT).

Iran: announced is not signed

June's pauses were calendar-shaped (June 11 to 21). Hormuz was closed on paper, then contested by Iran's own foreign ministry (FN13).

Energy is the transmission belt

Brent about $89 on June 12, about $80 on June 19, about $103 on Oct 30 (REPORTED). The 30-year sat at 5.02% in June.

Fulton

Georgia's June runoff was resolved. Younge v. Fulton DA is now on the Supreme Court calendar.

What we learned today
One actor, five moves

DOJ's Alaska suit, California ballots appeal, Washington refusal, poll-monitor declaration and the noon Saturday filing read as one pattern (INFERRED, about 3 of 6 outlets connect them).

The count is a calendar

Pennsylvania cannot pre-canvass until 7 AM Tuesday and cannot report before 8 PM (REPORTED law). A slow count is a known mechanism.

A record outbreak, low coverage

Ebola in DR Congo is the deadliest on record, about 2 of 6 outlets (REPORTED).

A broken chain

No boards exist for October and the county layer is blind. This exhibit is built on a federal and wire layer, flagged where degraded (FACT).

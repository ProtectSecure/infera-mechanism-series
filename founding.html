What the Founding 500 actually gets.

01

Private monthly thread with the editorial desk

One email a month, reply-all enabled, from the editor. We share what's in production, what we cut, and what we want field signal on. Subscribers can write back. We read everything.

02

Early access to drafts

Two weeks before public release, the cohort sees the draft of each episode and the methodology section. Comments are read by the editor before publication.

03

Named in the founding masthead

The first 500 readers are publicly listed on the masthead page at series.melophon.com/founding, first name and last initial only, opt-in. A permanent record of who showed up first.

04

Field-signal invitations

When we open new investigations, the cohort gets first invitation to contribute local intel: county records, broker tips, lived experience. Field signal moves the work.

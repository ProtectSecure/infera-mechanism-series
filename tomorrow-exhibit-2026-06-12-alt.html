PANEL I / THE LANCETS

Five lights, leaded in order

The tall lancets beneath the rose, read in the curator's order. Each carries its frozen planning gravity, a confidence stamp, and the next checkpoint. The two alert-now lights burn ruby; the war-economy lights burn cobalt.

I

The first FISA 702 lapse in the program's history

T01 · AUTHORITY LAPSES END OF DAYFACT (VOTES) / WATCH (MECHANICS)

The House failed a two-thirds suspension vote 198 to 218, three Senate unanimous-consent attempts were blocked, and both chambers left town until June 23. The renewal is hostage to Bill Pulte's acting-DNI appointment, and Trump says Pulte takes ODNI on June 19, inside the lapse window. The nearest analog is the June 2015 Section 215 sunset, but 2015 was a fight about the program; this is a fight about a personnel appointment with the program as hostage. Existing certifications likely run to expiry, so collection does not go dark at midnight; new directives stop.

92Alert now

NEXT CHECKPOINT: ODNI and DOJ continuity guidance; FISC filings; any White House movement on Pulte before June 19.

II

The Kharg Island threat

T02 · INTRADAY · PERSIAN GULF / WASHINGTONREPORTED / FACT (STRIKE RECORD)

The US said it is done with the latest round of strikes after the June 11 operations, then the President publicly threatened Kharg Island, the terminal handling about 90 percent of Iran's oil exports. The threat alone repriced crude intraday. Executed, it converts the energy shock into a supply catastrophe; not executed, it reads as pressure on the frozen-asset terms. A settlement is said to be reachable this weekend, but Iran says no deal is finalized and the blockade stays until the transaction is. Announced is not signed; treat Sunday as the outer edge of the resolution window.

90Alert now

NEXT CHECKPOINT: any signed settlement text; Kharg posture; crude at the open; an OFAC designation on Friday PI.

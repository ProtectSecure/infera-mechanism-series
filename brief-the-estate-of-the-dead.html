Not in that deal: the app, the chats and the characters people built, including personas of mothers, fathers and friends who have died. Those stay in the estate, and any other bidder can make an offer until November 20 at 5 p.m. The federal law that limits selling customer data protects the customer. It was not written for the dead.

02 THE DOOR WITH NO HANDLE · WHO DOES THE LAW SEE?

THE CUSTOMER HAS A DOOR.
THE DEAD DON'T.

Three people are tied to one memorial persona. Tap each to see which laws reach them. The legal readings are Infera's, from the statute text and reporting. Not legal advice. INFERENCE

11 U.S.C. §101(41A): personally identifiable information means information “provided by an individual to the debtor in connection with obtaining a product or a service from the debtor primarily for personal, family, or household purposes”, and covers information that identifies “such individual.” FACT govinfo

The statute protects the one who typed. Not the one who was typed about.

And one more clause: Character.AI's privacy policy reportedly reserves the right “to keep that Character active on the Services” after its creator deletes their account. A user who deletes everything to keep a parent's persona out of a sale may leave the public character behind, as an estate asset. REPORTED SAN INFERENCE

05 PRE-REGISTRATION · SCORED AT THE SALE HEARING

THREE WAYS THIS ENDS.
WE WROTE THEM DOWN FIRST.

Before the bid deadline, Infera names the branches and the signal for each. We will score this brief after the proposed November 24 sale hearing and publish whichever branch happened, including if it is one we did not list.

A
Someone bids for the people.

A buyer offers for the consumer app, chats or characters. Expect a motion for a consumer privacy ombudsman and the states' nine conditions in the sale order.

SIGNAL · a bid for “other assets” by Nov 20; ombudsman appointed by Nov 17
B
Only the license sells.

Disney's deal closes; the consumer platform is wound down. The question becomes a data destruction plan, and whether anyone asks what happens to personas of the dead.

SIGNAL · no qualified bid for other assets; a wind-down or destruction motion
C
The service keeps running.

A buyer keeps the app alive. Personas survive under a new owner's policy, with or without the states' conditions attached.

SIGNAL · a going-concern bid; an assignment of the privacy policy in the sale order

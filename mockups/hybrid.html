Why this hybrid

The Lens Console keeps Split-Reality's literacy point — that the same fact reads radically differently through different frameworks — and gives it the Board's broadcast affordances: geographic intelligence, live readings, switchable views, professional console feel.

The crucial move: clicking a lens doesn't just change the text. The map repaints, the vocabulary chips swap, the causal chain rearranges, the room itself tints with the lens's color. The reader experiences frameworks-as-environments, not framework-as-paragraph. They feel the difference, then they can read it.

Hybrid strengths: retains the literacy lesson; adds geographic intelligence; keyboard-driven (A/B/C/D); fast lens-switching builds intuition; the 'what this lens underplays' callout makes asymmetry of perspectives explicit. What to watch: requires per-lens map data per story (real editorial work, not just JS) and disciplined writing so the four lenses are genuinely distinct.

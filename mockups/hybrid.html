Material instability
"The system removes voters when their economic ground gives way."
The registration loss is downstream of the housing market. Rising rents, stagnant wages, and a tight inventory produce eviction filings, which displace households, which break the address-on-file pipeline that registration depends on. The civic harm is a knock-on effect of a market failure.

What changed in v2

The map was decoration. Replaced. Each lens now generates a different visualization type, not a recolored version of the same chart. The Economic lens shows a household balance sheet over time. The Political lens shows a six-gate statutory funnel. The Media lens shows 24-month story salience with the eviction pipeline conspicuously flat. The Cultural lens shows generational voter-persistence cohort curves.

Each visualization is the lens's argument. The Economic balance-sheet argues that the mechanism is a financial crossing-point. The Political funnel argues it's a statutorily designed attrition. The Media salience chart argues it's a story the news system can't tell. The Cultural cohort chart argues it's intergenerational decay. Same fact, four genuinely different information designs.

This is the intelligent information design move: the visualization for each interpretation is in that interpretation's native language, not a generic widget recolored four times.

Why this direction

The Intelligence Board makes the explanatory choice operational. The reader becomes the producer at the desk, picking a lens and watching the map respond. It's the closest analog to live broadcast analysis — Kornacki at the board — applied to interpretive uncertainty rather than vote counts.

Strengths: high-utility, professional feel; great for newsroom partnerships; visually dramatic; obvious commercial license potential.
Weaknesses: requires actual per-county data per lens; demands editorial discipline so the lenses aren't just visual recoloring of the same metric.

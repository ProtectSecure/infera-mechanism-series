Personhood, on lease.

An investigation into the invisible economy that runs on you — what it costs, who collects, and where the money quietly returns.

Act 02Setup

The thing you are
quietly worried about.

The conflict at the center of this issue — named before any frame is built around it.

You understand, abstractly, that data is collected. What you can’t see is the architecture: how an image becomes a forecast, the forecast becomes a cluster, the cluster becomes inventory, the inventory becomes a data center you help pay to power, and the AI hosted on that data center charges you a monthly fee to use a model trained — in part — on what you gave away for free. You are simultaneously the product, the labor, and the closing payer.

Act 03Architecture

Three moves
that make personhood
monetizable.

The pipeline is not hidden. It is unwatched. Each chapter below is one of the three structural moves the entire industry rests on.
Ch · 01

Read every image twice.

First for what is there. Then for what it suggests.

Modern vision systems no longer ask what is this? — they ask who is this becoming? Forty-seven inferred attributes per uploaded frame: not just objects, but emotional tone, life-stage, purchase posture, network density, openness, stability.

47 signalsper uploaded frame · across 12 inferred attribute classes
Ch · 02

Fuse twelve sources
into one composite.

No platform alone knows you. The composite does.

Instagram, LinkedIn, streaming, banking, smart home, location, public records, wearables, broker fusion. Twelve channels meet inside a single identity ledger. The result is not a copy of you — it is a forecast accurate enough to underwrite credit and steer purchase.

312 attributesper typical composite · updated continuously · never shown to the subject
Ch · 03

Cluster everyone
into six shadows.

Personhood, compressed into six recurring shapes the model has learned to price.

Curator, Caretaker, Drifter, Believer, Hustler, Apprentice. Each cluster carries its own bid floor, its own predicted trajectory, its own end-state. The trajectory is not a prediction. It is increasingly a prescription.

14,400+named lookalike clusters in production globally · addressing nearly every adult

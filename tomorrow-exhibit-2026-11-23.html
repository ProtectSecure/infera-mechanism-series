Gallery one · Field noteA quiet Monday that is not quiet

Monday opens on the first session after the S&P 500 entered a correction, and the Gulf produced a direct US and Iran exchange on Friday. Washington is otherwise quiet: no House or Senate committee meetings, no Supreme Court argument, no posted White House schedule. So the record moves on paper. The Federal Register prints the Labor Department's independent contractor rule at 8:45 AM, and two states close their 2026 books inside 24 hours: North Carolina's recount deadline, with control of the state Senate in play, and Pennsylvania's county certification deadline.

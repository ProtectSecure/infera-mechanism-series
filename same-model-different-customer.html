Room 1 · Five machines

One fact about you. Five different prices.

Tap a fact about a person. Each machine below shows how a system of its kind would read that same fact. The top of each card is what the home exhibit documents about the machine. The reading underneath is our illustration of how such a system uses a signal like this, and is marked that way.

Readings are illustrations of documented model types, not claims about any named company's treatment of any person. INFERENCE

Room 2 · The point

The model is the same. The customer is what changes.

The patternA sportsbook's "elasticity," a consultant's "capacity to give," a platform's ranking and a lender's score all do one thing: estimate what a person is worth to the institution, from what the person does. None of the five asks what the person needs. INFERENCEcuriosity engine, through line 2

The other side

  • Scoring is how a small staff serves many people: a church finds who has drifted, a bank catches fraud, an app shows you what you like.
  • The same late-night signal that sells a bet can trigger a welfare check. What matters is which model is switched on. INFERENCE

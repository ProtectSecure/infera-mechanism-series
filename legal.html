Privacy

Plain version: we collect almost nothing, and we don't share what little we collect.

What we collect when you visit

  • Server logs from our host (Render.com): IP address, user-agent, page requested, timestamp. Retained 30 days for security and debugging. Not joined to any identity.
  • Aggregate, cookieless measurement. The site counts page visits with Plausible Analytics: no cookies, no persistent identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting, aggregate counts only — we own the data and it is never joined to any identity. Still no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising cookies, no ad tech of any kind.
  • Browser local-storage. We use first-party browser local-storage for: the Personhood Inc persona memory (cross-episode echoes), evidence-mode preference, episode reading-log (the Case File feature), and signup intent. None of this leaves your device. Clear it any time at case-file.html.

What we collect if you subscribe

Your email address, the date you subscribed, the page you subscribed from, and any tag (e.g., founding-500) we applied at signup. Subscriptions on the current site are submitted directly to infera@melophon.com via your mail client; we keep the list manually and do not run third-party tracking on the form.

What we collect if you email us

Your email and whatever you sent us, plus our reply. Stored in Google Workspace (Gmail), governed by Google's privacy terms.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or share your email with anyone, for any reason
  • Build advertising audiences from your reading behavior
  • Cross-reference visitors with broker profiles (this would be hypocritical given what we cover)
  • Embed tracking pixels in our email
  • Use cookies for behavioral targeting

Your rights

  • Access: write to infera@melophon.com and ask what we have on you. We respond within five days.
  • Deletion: click unsubscribe in any newsletter, or write to infera@melophon.com.
  • Correction: write to infera@melophon.com.
  • Portability: your Case File is downloadable as JSON directly from case-file.html.
  • EU / UK readers: you have the rights described in Articles 15–22 of the GDPR. We honor all of them.
  • California readers: you have the rights described in CCPA §§ 1798.100–199. We honor all of them.
  • Children: the site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, write to infera@melophon.com for immediate deletion.

Corrections

Corrections are the most important email we get. Send them to infera@melophon.com with: the specific claim, the specific episode, and supporting evidence.

What happens next

  • Acknowledged within 48 hours
  • Reviewed within 5 business days
  • Published as a dated correction note on the affected episode if we agree
  • Published as a dated response with reasoning if we disagree
  • Confidence stamp on the original claim updated as appropriate

Right of response

Any person, entity, or official named in an episode may submit a request for clarification or right-of-response to the same address. We review every such request fairly and on the merits. We do not unpublish journalism; we correct it.

DMCA · copyright takedown

If you believe content on this site infringes your copyright, send a DMCA notice to infera@melophon.com with all of the following:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  2. Identification of the allegedly infringing material on our site, including the URL
  3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone, email)
  4. A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized
  5. A statement under penalty of perjury that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf
  6. Your physical or electronic signature

We respond within the statutory window. Counter-notices follow 17 U.S.C. § 512(g) procedure.

Notice: Knowingly making a material misrepresentation in a DMCA notice or counter-notice may subject you to liability for damages, including costs and attorney's fees, under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

Accessibility

The Mechanism Series is committed to WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.

What we do

  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion globally: animations drop to near-zero duration on request
  • Use semantic HTML throughout
  • Maintain visible keyboard focus on every interactive element
  • Test text contrast at AA minimum for body text
  • Provide a "Skim" reading mode on every Season I episode
  • Strip canvas-heavy motion on small viewports

Where we fall short

  • Some interactive visualizations are not fully screen-reader-navigable yet; we are working on this
  • Mobile rendering is improving episode by episode; not all motion is fully stripped on small screens
  • Older browsers may render the chassis with reduced fidelity

Report an issue

Write to infera@melophon.com with the page URL, your browser and device, and a brief description. We respond within five business days.

Editorial disclosures

Methodology

Every load-bearing claim carries a confidence stamp (Strong, Plausible, Correlated, Competing) and a provenance badge (Documented, Modeled, Speculative). Sources are tiered T1 (primary official) through T4 (contextual commentary). The complete claim catalog with sources is at the Claims Atlas.

Sources policy

We link to the most specific publicly accessible page for every cited claim. We do not pay for sources. We do not grant anonymity to sources whose claims we cannot independently corroborate. We re-verify all cited URLs on every site deploy.

Conflicts of interest

The Infera editorial desk discloses any material conflict on the relevant episode. Material conflicts include: a staff editor's direct financial interest in a named subject; a close personal or familial relationship with a named decision-maker; an underwriter's commercial relationship with a named subject. Staff editors do not own individual stocks in any company that could appear in our investigations. Diversified index funds are not a conflict.

Independence and funding

Infera is independent. No advertiser pays us. No platform deal exists. No political party, candidate, advocacy organization, lobbying firm, or government agency funds the publication. The series is supported by the reader newsletter, occasional research engagements, and a small number of named foundations underwriting specific Georgia field work. Underwriters do not see episodes prior to publication. Editorial decisions are made independently of underwriting decisions, by different people.

Use of AI

The Mechanism Series uses AI tools for code generation, data processing, and editorial drafting assistance. All editorial decisions, claims, sourcing, and final language are reviewed and approved by human editors before publication. AI does not author episodes.

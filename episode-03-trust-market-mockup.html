WHERE THIS IS HEADING · THE END GOAL

The next decade isn't about more data.
It's about fewer humans in the decision.

A single composite score
Hiring + insurance + lending + housing + dating fuse into one cross-sector reputation token. Privately held, opaque, mostly accurate, occasionally devastating.
Autonomous decision agents
Bidders, recommenders, underwriters, recruiters — all AI agents transacting on your composite without any human in the chain until it's already locked in.
The quiet denial
You won't be told no. The interview just doesn't come. The rate is slightly higher. The match never shows up. The credit is approved but at a different number than your neighbor.

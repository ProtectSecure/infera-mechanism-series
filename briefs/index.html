BRIEF № 004 MON · JUN 15 · 2026 VOL I · THE HIDDEN CLOCKS
The Reassessment Clock
Allegheny County last reassessed property values on May 18, 2012. That was 5,142 days ago. North Carolina law requires every county to reassess every 8 years. Pennsylvania law requires nothing. When a county lets the years pile up, the unfairness compounds: fast-appreciating neighborhoods underpay, slow-appreciating ones overpay, school districts get distorted revenue. This is a clock that runs backwards.

How the Brief fits into the Series

The Mechanism Brief is the daily editorial product in Infera's format taxonomy. It draws from the operational layer (The Desk) and turns upcoming civic mechanisms into stories the reader can act on. It's shorter than a Special Report and faster than a Field Note. It always ends with what to do.

Briefs are organized into Volumes by thematic arc. Volume I, currently open, follows the hidden clocks: time-bound civic mechanisms affecting ordinary people in rooms nobody is watching. Each Brief is one clock. When the clock arc exhausts, Volume II opens on a different theme.

If you want the raw public schedule rather than the synthesized brief, see The Desk. If you want monthly analytical dispatches, see Field Notes. If you want the long-form chassis, see Episodes.

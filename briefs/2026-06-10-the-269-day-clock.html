BRIEF № 002· WEDNESDAY · JUNE 10 · 2026· ≈ 9 MIN READ· FREE PUBLIC

Pennsylvania has two hundred and sixty nine days. Then a hospital's records are gone.

A bankruptcy court has ordered the destruction of every Crozer Health System patient record after March 2, 2027. That is 269 days from today. Atlanta Medical Center patients had 62. The math is not the point. What happens to the records is the point, and the point has a deadline.

EXHIBIT BDID YOU KNOW · A POP QUIZ

You've watched two American hospital systems close in the last four years. Was the math the same?

Three questions. The answers are quietly devastating. Take a guess before tapping a tile, then see what actually happened.

DYK 01ATLANTA MEDICAL CENTER · GEORGIA · 2022
How many days of notice did Atlanta Medical Center patients get before the doors closed?
ANSWER · 62 DAYS

Wellstar announced the closure August 31, 2022. The hospital shut November 1.

That's roughly 9 weeks for one of metro Atlanta's two Level-I trauma centers — and the only Level-I serving downtown Atlanta — to wind down. The decision triggered emergency meetings of the Fulton County Hospital Authority, the City of Atlanta, and the State of Georgia. None of those meetings changed the closure date.

SOURCE · Wellstar Health System closure notice · Aug 31, 2022. Atlanta-Journal Constitution timeline coverage.
DYK 02WHAT HAPPENED TO THE RECORDS
What happened to Atlanta Medical Center patients' medical records after the closure?
ANSWER · KEPT BY WELLSTAR

AMC was inside a going-concern health system. Wellstar absorbed the records and kept them accessible through its existing patient portal.

This is the quiet difference. Wellstar didn't go bankrupt — it closed an unprofitable hospital but continued operating. Federal HIPAA retention rules (6 years minimum, longer for minors and certain conditions) applied through Wellstar's living infrastructure. The records survived because the company did.

SOURCE · HIPAA retention rules · 45 CFR § 164.530(j) · Wellstar patient records portal.
DYK 03CROZER HEALTH SYSTEM · PENNSYLVANIA · 2026
If Crozer patients have 269 days from today, why is this worse than Atlanta's 62?
ANSWER · NO LIVING INFRASTRUCTURE

Prospect Medical filed Chapter 11. The bankruptcy court treats the records as an estate liability, not a healthcare asset.

The court approved destruction because warehousing them costs money the estate doesn't have. Morgan Records Management was designated as a single point of contact to manage the wind-down. After March 2, 2027, the storage contract ends and the destruction begins. There's no Wellstar to inherit them. There's no surviving company at all.

SOURCE · Prospect Medical bankruptcy · Case 3725 · Omni Agent Solutions · Delaware County PA update page.

EXHIBIT CWHERE THE RECORDS GO

Two paths leave Prospect's filing cabinet. One survives the deadline.

In May 2025, ChristianaCare purchased a subset of outpatient assets and inherited their imaging records. Those survive. Everything else is on a different path. Watch the dots travel.

The red path is roughly 95% of the volume. The green path is the imaging records ChristianaCare bought. Most records were never imaging records. If you went to Crozer for cardiac, oncology, primary care, labor & delivery, behavioral health, or anything else that wasn't a scan, you're in the red.

01IS THIS ABOUT YOU?

Three quick checks. You'll know in 15 seconds.

Crozer Health System operated five hospitals in Delaware County and surrounding Philadelphia suburbs for over 100 years. Crozer-Chester Medical Center was the region's largest non-Penn-affiliated medical center. If you, your parents, or your children lived in southeast Pennsylvania between roughly 1990 and 2024, the chance you have records in this estate is not small.

Did you, or anyone in your household, ever receive care at Crozer-Chester, Delaware County Memorial, Springfield Hospital, Taylor Hospital, or Community Hospital?
Did you give birth at a Crozer facility (Delaware County had hundreds of Crozer-delivered babies per year)?
Were you treated for cardiac, oncology, or behavioral-health conditions at Crozer? (Specialties Crozer was known for.)

02WHAT TO ACTUALLY DO

If you, your parents, or your kids ever went to Crozer for anything, do this now.

The phrase "you have nine months" sounds like a lot. It is not. Records management requests at scale take time. The vendor is going to be busy. The system gets slower as the deadline approaches. Move now.

STEP 01

Request your Crozer medical records

Morgan Records Management is the single approved vendor for all Crozer record requests. They handle the paperwork. They are the official channel.

crozer.morganrecordsmanagement.com/request-my-crozer-records.html
STEP 02

Watch Delaware County's update page

The county updates the bankruptcy page when the records-management process or deadline changes. Bookmark it. Check monthly until you have your records in hand.

delcopa.gov/health/prospect-medical-bankruptcy-update
STEP 03

If you were a Crozer employee, check the employment-support section

The county's page also covers behavioral-health support and employment support for affected Crozer workers. The labor side of a hospital closure has its own deadline.

STEP 04

If you want to follow the litigation

The bankruptcy case dockets are public at the Omni Agent Solutions portal. Case ID 3725. Filings appear there before press coverage.

cases.omniagentsolutions.com/?clientId=3725

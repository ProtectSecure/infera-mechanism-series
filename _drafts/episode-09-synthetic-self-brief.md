## What changed in v2, and why

The June draft argued from mechanism alone. Since then the publication has *documented cases*, so this episode now reports instead of predicting:

- **Field Note № 12 The Ventriloquist** found the dead already campaigning (Tamil Nadu 2024, Indonesia 2024), already lobbying (The Shotline, 2024), already preaching (the Kirk voice clip in three churches, September 2025). The "replica for someone else's purpose" layer is no longer hypothetical.
- **Grandma, Updated** put prices on intimacy: subscription tiers, store currencies, blind-draw cards, and a griefbot whose FAQ says a family "can keep paying to maintain access." That is the Subscription Self thesis in one sentence, so EP 10 folds in here.
- **The Second Death** established that the evidence base is thin (no randomized trials found) and that harms cluster at *ending*, not at starting.

The merge makes one sharper argument than two episodes did: **the synthetic you is cheap to make, and the real you is expensive to keep. The same market sells both.**

## Compression Point

> ***"The machine no longer needs you online to deploy you. It only needs someone to keep paying."***

**Reader-arrival framing:** *Eight episodes assumed you were in the room: scored, fed, sorted, composited. EP 09 removes you. A version of you can now campaign, preach, sell and console without you. Both columns of this ledger end in the same place: if you stop paying, someone else holds the identity.*

### PIPELINE · *Recognition* · Two ledgers, one market

The v1 "three layers" survive, now set as a ledger with a **debit** side (what is made of you) and a **rent** side (what it costs to stay you).

**Debit ledger · the replica**

1. **For someone else's purpose.** Campaign speech, pulpit audio, fraud calls. Engine: voice cloning at falling input cost. Persuasion evidence: in experiments with 76,977 UK participants across 19 models, post-training raised persuasiveness by up to 51%, with lower factual accuracy as persuasion rose (FACT, Hackenburg et al., Science, Dec 2025).
2. **For your own purpose.** Agents that schedule, negotiate and message as you. Convenience is real; binding decisions you did not personally make are the unsettled part.
3. **For the people who survive you.** Griefbots and "versonas." Handed off to EP 10.

**Rent ledger · the original** (the former Subscription Self, compressed)

1. **The cost of being recognized.** Verification and KYC services that log you into banks, agencies and clinics.
2. **The cost of being recoverable.** Password managers, backups, takeover recovery.
3. **The cost of being continuous.** Every move, job change, name change and phone change is reconciliation labor.
4. **New in v2 · the cost of being remembered.** Companion and memorial products price intimacy in tiers. Replika's store sells items that "change your Replika's personality" (FACT, help center). Character.AI's c.ai+ runs $9.99 a month (REPORTED). Chinese companion apps sell blind-draw "cards" of a companion (REPORTED). HereAfter: a family "can keep paying to maintain access" (FACT, FAQ).

The hinge sentence of the episode: *the rent ledger's fourth line is the debit ledger's third line, seen from the family's side.* The bill for remembering you is paid by the people who loved you.

### OUTCOME · *Implication*

Three populations, carried over from v1 and sharpened:

1. **The originals.** Their voices are inputs. Most never consented, because most were never asked. The Pelkey vacatur is the first appellate signal that a court will distinguish "what the family believed he would say" from his own voice (REPORTED, as published in The Second Death).
2. **The targets.** Voters, congregations, the grandparent on the phone. The FCC treats AI voices as "artificial" under the TCPA (FACT, Feb 8, 2024). Thirty-three states list enacted election deepfake laws (FACT, Public Citizen tracker count, Oct 21, 2026).
3. **The payers.** Households whose subscriptions lapse in a shock. *Callback to EP 08 The Class Engine:* the class the machine sorted you into decides which rent tier you can hold. *Callback to S1·01 The Trickle:* wages are the denominator.

Named directly: **the deployable synthetic is an asset class, and the coherent original is a subscription.** Neither is owned by the person they describe.

### COMPETING · *Systems Realization (one half)*

Hosted fairly, five defenses (v1's four plus the subscription defense):

1. **Useful agency.** Agents free attention. True for many users.
2. **Grief and advocacy.** The Shotline families chose the voices and labeled them. Manuel Oliver says he is not trying to bring his son back (REPORTED, Semafor).
3. **Creative and estate-licensed use.** ElevenLabs' Iconic Marketplace requires rights-holder approval per use (REPORTED); the Tupac estate forced a track down within days (REPORTED, CBC).
4. **Consent regimes are maturing.** California AB 1836, New York's 40-year post-mortem term, Tennessee's ELVIS Act, the EU AI Act Article 50 disclosure duty enforceable since Aug 2, 2026 (all REPORTED).
5. **Subscriptions buy security.** Continuous patching and recovery cost money; free tiers exist; self-managed identity fails more often for non-experts.

### DISCIPLINE · *Systems Realization (other half)*

Refusals: no mind-control framing (persuasion effects are measured in points, not possession); no inevitability; no pathologizing of consenting use; no "racket" framing of subscriptions.

What we did NOT prove:
- That any named platform built a specific synthetic without consent.
- That AI voices of the dead changed any election outcome. No 2026 midterm ad using a resurrected dead person was found (WATCH).
- That identity-stack cost is rising faster than wages for most households (needs population data).

What we did prove:
- Documented, dated uses of the voices of the dead in politics, advocacy and worship across three countries.
- That input requirements for a credible replica have fallen by orders of magnitude.
- That remembrance is priced in tiers, and that at least one memorial product makes continued access a family payment.

## Interactive Core · The Two Ledgers

**Stage 1 · YOUR SURFACE.** Sliders for hours of public voice, video minutes, posts. A dial reads *not credible / short-form deception / sustained impersonation*. New in v2: the dial's thresholds are anchored to the documented 3-hours-to-10-seconds collapse, and a ghost needle shows where the threshold sat in 2022.

**Stage 2 · YOUR RENT.** The reader ticks the services that keep them coherent and enters monthly costs. A fifth row, *remembrance*, is pre-filled with the real price points from Grandma, Updated's Grief Store, so the reader sees what their family's line would look like.

**Stage 3 · THE CROSSOVER.** The two ledgers meet in one line: *cost to make a replica of you* against *cost to keep the real you for a year*. When the replica is cheaper (it usually is), the page says so plainly and links to The Shotline as the counter-example of a replica made *right*.

Closing reveal: *"The replica gets cheaper every year. The original never does."*

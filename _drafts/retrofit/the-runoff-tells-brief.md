## The eight fields

1. **Reader question.** What can a primary runoff show about who holds a coalition together, and what can it not show?
2. **Contribution.**
   - Existing coverage establishes the slate and SB 202's shorter window (MIT Election Lab, GPB, AJC).
   - Infera sets the runoff's turnout history beside four competing lenses on the same fact.
   - Since the retrofit, it says plainly that each lens is a hypothesis.
3. **Human situation.** Voters asked to return a second time in 28 days.
   - Reporting basis: results and studies at a distance.
   - Reader entry point: a ballot you might skip.
4. **Form.**
   - Dominant: mechanism investigation.
   - Supporting: comparison (four lenses).

6. **Competing explanation.**
   - The core question: is turnout drop-off organized targeting, or the ordinary cost of voting twice?
   - What would discriminate: precinct drop-off by income, and voter-file return rates.
   - The page now names this question instead of answering it.
7. **Agency.**
   - People: voters decide whether to return.
   - Institutions: the legislature set the window (SB 202).
   - Reader: can read results knowing who came back.
   - Lever Map: window length is the lever, and the legislature holds it.
8. **Ending and boundary.**
   - The page ends on four open lenses and a status box.
   - Out of scope: whether any campaign ran targeting. That needs its own evidence.
   - Stop rule: certified turnout by precinct.

## Checks

- **Early frame check (retro).** The commission premise assumed its answer: the June 2 editorial note asserted a targeting infrastructure.
  - Entman cause: asserted with no evidence. Now "not established."
  - Single-frame risk: the bridge and the note supplied all four functions from Melophon's own assertion.
  - Hall oppositional reading: "Melophon is calling a normal election rigged." It was plausible and unanswered. It is now answered by the withdrawal.
- **Precession test.** Not applicable: no model or market shapes the runoff on this page.
- **Variety check.** Its four-lens console repeats the series' modality, but its question (what a second ballot reveals) is its own.
- **Consent ledger.** Only public candidates and officials appear. No private persons. No images of real people.
- **Quotation check.** The four lens taglines and the "shared fact" sat in quotation marks without being quotes. Quotation marks removed.

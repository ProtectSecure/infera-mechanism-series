NAPKIN 1 · ABQAIQ, SEPTEMBER 2019
Drones knocked out 5% of world oil supply in one morning, the biggest single disruption ever. Brent jumped 15% at the open... and gave nearly all of it back within weeks once supply proved repairable. The lesson for tonight: opens overstate, repairs decide.
NAPKIN 2 · WHY THIS IS NOT 1973
The embargo era ran on someone choosing not to sell. Tonight runs on whether tankers can physically pass a strait, while the US is a net exporter and strategic reserves exist on every continent. Same anxiety, different machine. Price the chokepoint, not the memory.
NAPKIN 3 · THE 2018 PRECEDENT
Iran has threatened to close Hormuz before, loudly, in 2018, and never attempted it, because the strait is also Iran's own export artery. A closure starves the treasury that orders it. That is why Friday's strike on the mine bunkers matters more than the threat itself.
NAPKIN 4 · SUNDAY OPENS, THE HOUSE STATISTIC
Weekend gaps in crude have a habit the regulars know: thin Sunday volume exaggerates, Monday Europe corrects. The honest read of tonight arrives with London's open at 3 AM ET. Set an alarm or trust the cream soda, your call.

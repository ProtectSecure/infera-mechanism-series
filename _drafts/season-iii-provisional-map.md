## The bridge, corrected

The May map assumed Season II would close with a convergence essay. What actually happened: EP 08 *The Class Engine* closed the machine (Acts I and II), and Season II now ends with a three-part Act III, *Past You*: EP 09 The Synthetic Self, EP 10 The Afterlife of Your Data, EP 11 The Governance Layer, with the reader's Lever Map as Part 12.

The four Season III questions survive, and Season II has now given each one a **seed case**: a dated, documented situation the episode can open on, rather than a thought experiment.

> *What is the worst that could happen?*
> *What is the best?*
> *Who is currently managing this?*
> *What is actually possible now?*

Each Season III episode opens with a callback to its seed, so a returning reader recognizes the case and a new reader gets the scaffolding.

## Six provisional episodes

### S3·01 · The Worst Case

**Compression point:** *"The worst case isn't a villain. It's a building."* (kept)
**Seed case (new):** a bankrupt AI company whose estate holds 163 million accounts' worth of conversations, where users are told they are not creditors and no ombudsman has been appointed (Brief 007, REPORTED/WATCH). Add the buildings: EP 07 The Physical Plant's data centers, where the conversations physically sit.
**Why the seed sharpens it:** the honest worst case is administrative, not cinematic. Not malice. A sale order, a storage contract and a courtroom where nobody speaks for the people inside the data.
**Discipline:** every projection labeled; bounded by documented capability.

### S3·02 · The Best Case

**Compression point:** *"The best case is the thing no government has built yet."* (kept)
**Seed cases (new), each already operating somewhere:**
- A data directive that outranks terms of service (France, art. 85).
- A bankruptcy sale with conditions on the buyer (23andMe).
- Consent and labels by design (The Shotline: each family consented, every call labeled).
- Translation reaching languages that never had scripture (Faith Layer 09: 544 languages still waiting, down from 985 a year earlier).
**Discipline:** a "what already works somewhere" episode, sourced to real institutions.

### S3·03 · The Custodian Gap

**Compression point:** *"Everyone is managing a slice. No one is managing the whole."* (kept)
**Seed cases (new):**
- The Special Report's four-room ledger (*who was in the room?* thinning from hospital to companion app).
- The Shadow Market line now in EP 10: *"governed in pieces, by many actors, with no integrator."*
- The parish vault in The Second Death: a congregation asked to keep its members' digital remains with no rule to follow.
**Closer (kept):** *"The job description for the integrator exists. The job is open."*

### S3·04 · What You Can Actually Do

**Compression point:** *"You cannot opt out. You can opt in to specific things that work."* (kept)
**Seed set (new): the take-home objects Season II and the Faith Layer already built.** Before I Go card (The Second Death), Digital Life Card (Guardian Angel Inc.), the check-in data policy (Check-In), the stream dial (The Quiet Guest), Part 12 Lever Map (EP 11), and the export-before-the-sale habit (Brief 007: exports take three to four weeks). The episode calibrates each: effect size, cost, ongoing commitment, which slice of the mechanism it touches.
**Discipline:** 8 to 12 actions, calibrated. Neither defeatism nor a 47-item list.

### S3·05 · The Aspirational Engine

**Compression point:** *"The Joneses are an algorithm now."* (kept)
**Seed case (new):** Grandma, Updated's *as she was / as we wish* slider. Sanctification of the dead is a documented grief pattern (Lopata 1981); the aspirational engine applies the same edit to the living, selling a better self by subscription. Bridge to Faith Layer 07: the hours a Sabbath empties are the hours this engine fills.

### S3·06 · The Price You Don't See

**Compression point:** *"Same store, same product, different price. You weren't supposed to notice."* (kept)
**Seed cases (new):** Field Note № 07 *The Fast Lane* (a $750 paid lane to the front of a public queue); the Grief Store's tiers, store currencies and blind-draw cards (Grandma, Updated); and the two bills for one building (Same Building, Two Bills).

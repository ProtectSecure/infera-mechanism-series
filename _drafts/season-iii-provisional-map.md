## What the Faith Layer changed

v2 drew its seed cases mostly from Season II. The Faith Layer turned out to be the sharpest test of every Season III question, because a congregation is the smallest institution that holds the most intimate data under the fewest rules. Four findings now anchor the season:

1. **The most intimate records sit outside every privacy law.** HIPAA covers only health plans, clearinghouses and providers who bill electronically (45 CFR 160.103, FACT). Prayer requests, counseling notes and children's check-in records usually fall outside HIPAA, COPPA, FERPA and below every state privacy threshold (Exhibit 07, FACT and INFERENCE).
2. **Privilege covers the words, not the file.** Courts have ordered church files produced because privilege protects confidential spiritual communication, not an institution's records (Allentown 1997; A.O. v. Diocese of Brooklyn, Dec 2025, FACT). A chatbot log has no privilege at all, by the industry's best-known chief executive's account (Exhibit 02, REPORTED).
3. **People cannot see their own hours.** Self-reported and logged screen time correlate only moderately (r = 0.38), and the errors run both ways (Parry et al. 2021, FACT). The traditions' own texts set the bar for persuasion at freedom and time to reflect, not effectiveness (Exhibit 06, FACT).
4. **Nobody is the integrator, and the missing number proves it.** Zero of ECFA's seven accreditation standards mention data (FACT). We found no survey of how many US congregations have a written privacy policy (Exhibit 07, INFERENCE). Germany's churches run their own data law under independent oversight; US congregations have nothing comparable (REPORTED).

## Six provisional episodes

### S3·01 · The Worst Case
**Compression point:** *"The worst case isn't a villain. It's a building."*
**Seeds:**
- Brief 007: a bankrupt AI company whose estate holds chats and personas of the dead until a bid deadline.
- EP 07 The Physical Plant: the buildings where those conversations physically sit.
- **New, Exhibit 07:** the bankruptcy-side breach in the Baltimore archdiocese case, where hacks at the creditors' committee's adviser and law firm put abuse survivors' data at risk; and the Seoul megachurch theft with reported traces of AI agents.
- **New, Exhibit 02:** a teenager's seven sentences at 2 a.m., routed to a company log, a training pipeline and "a court, if asked."
**Sharpened:** the honest worst case is administrative and quiet: a sale order, a storage contract, a claims agent, a courtroom where nobody speaks for the people inside the data.

### S3·02 · The Best Case
**Compression point:** *"The best case is the thing no government has built yet."*
**Seeds, each already operating somewhere:**
- France's data directive that outranks terms of service; the 23andMe sale with conditions on the buyer.
- The Shotline: consent and labels by design (Field Note 12).
- **New, Exhibit 07:** churches that run their own data law under independent oversight (GDPR Article 91; Germany's Catholic and Protestant church data laws).
- **New, Exhibit 06:** New York's quiet hours for minors from 25 January 2027; prayer apps that let users hide streaks.
- **New, Exhibit 02:** a pastor's AI twin that answers "Call the pastor" on suicide or abuse: a handback built in.
- Language: Bible translation still waiting to begin in 544 languages, down from 985 a year earlier (Wycliffe; material held from the shelved To All Nations concept).

### S3·03 · The Custodian Gap
**Compression point:** *"Everyone is managing a slice. No one is managing the whole."*
**Seeds:**
- The Special Report's four-room ledger; EP 10's line, "governed in pieces, by many actors, with no integrator."
- **New, Exhibit 07:** ECFA's 0 of 7; the missing survey; private equity ownership of church-software vendors, so member records move under a vendor's terms, not the congregation's promises.
- **New, Exhibit 07 Room D:** the Belgian baptism case, where the question of who governs a church's register is now before the EU Court of Justice.
- The Second Death's parish vault.
**Closer (kept):** *"The job description for the integrator exists. The job is open."*

### S3·04 · What You Can Actually Do
**Compression point:** *"You cannot opt out. You can opt in to specific things that work."*
**Seed set: the take-home objects, now complete.**
- The Faith Layer charter: seven clauses, each with a place the rule already exists (Exhibit 07).
- The Digital Life Card (Exhibit 02), the Before I Go card (Exhibit 05), the nursery policy (Exhibit 03), the stream dial (Exhibit 01), the persuasion line (Exhibit 06).
- Part 12 Lever Map (EP 11); export before the sale (Brief 007).
**New calibration evidence (Exhibit 06):** stepping away helps a little (under a tenth of a standard deviation in the two largest experiments); letting people set their own future limits cuts use substantially. The episode should rank actions by that kind of measured effect.

### S3·05 · The Aspirational Engine
**Compression point:** *"The Joneses are an algorithm now."*
**Seeds:**
- Grandma, Updated's *as she was / as we wish* slider.
- **New, Exhibit 06:** the drift from a morning psalm to an ad in one feed; autoplay as the feature users most often said left them less in control; the persuasion ladder from invitation to deception.
**Bridge:** the hours a Sabbath empties are the hours this engine fills (Exhibit 06 Room B).

### S3·06 · The Price You Don't See
**Compression point:** *"Same store, same product, different price. You weren't supposed to notice."*
**Seeds:**
- Field Note 07 The Fast Lane; the Grief Store's tiers (Grandma, Updated); Same Building, Two Bills.
- **New, Exhibit 06:** faith-app subscriptions and a campaign funnel reported at about 30,000 reading-plan sign-ups from roughly 100 million people exposed; a church-data firm reported to sell leads to churches by the month.

## Inspection lenses (new)

Seven questions any page in the series can be held up to. The Systems Atlas lets a reader switch each one on and see how every narrative answers it: *Who holds the file? Who can ask for it? When does it end? Who consented? Who pays? Who integrates? Where did the time go?* Season III episodes each own one or two lenses: S3·01 (who can ask), S3·02 (who consented), S3·03 (who integrates), S3·04 (when does it end), S3·05 (where did the time go), S3·06 (who pays). *Who holds the file* runs through all six.

## Length

## What Season III is not (kept)

Not solutions. Not advocacy. Not techno-optimism or techno-pessimism; worst and best are paired.

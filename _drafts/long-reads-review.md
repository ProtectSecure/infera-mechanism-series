Stated times include time spent on instruments, so some gap is right. A stated 40 to 50 minutes still tells a reader not to start.

## Done now, on every one of these (site kit)
- A progress line and a "minutes left" pill, so the length is never a surprise.
- A Chapters menu built from the page's own headings.
- Resume: the page remembers the reader's place on their device and offers to pick up there.
- On reads over 25 minutes, an intermission card at the heading nearest the midpoint.
- A keep-going card and a subscribe strip at the end.

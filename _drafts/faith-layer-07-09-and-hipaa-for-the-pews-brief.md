## Exhibit 07 · A Palace in Time

**Compression point:** *"The feed was built to fill the hours a Sabbath was built to empty."*

**Rooms (from the Sabbath dossier):**
1. **Place your Sabbath.** A 168-hour radial week. The reader drags a 24-hour rest block onto it; overlays show what a feed is designed to fill: teens' late-night phone use on school nights (50.1 minutes, 10pm to 6am, FACT, verify DOI), half of teens studied getting 237+ notifications a day (FACT, Common Sense), and Sunday as the heaviest phone day (REPORTED, survey, directional).
2. **Same hooks, different altar.** Streaks, push and variable rewards in social apps (as alleged in AG complaints) beside the same patterns inside prayer apps. Hallow's Ash Wednesday 2026 spike, about 263,000 downloads (FACT, estimate). Both sides: a streak that gets someone to pray is still a streak.
3. **The detox ladder.** A forest plot of real effect sizes, small ones included: Facebook and Instagram deactivation gains of 0.06 and 0.04 SD (FACT, Allcott et al. 2025); blocking mobile internet cut daily screen time from 314 to 161 minutes (FACT, PNAS Nexus 2025). Honest finding: rest helps a little, measurably.
4. **From blue laws to quiet hours.** Collective rest as law: Israel's 2001 Shabbat elevator rule (FACT), Australia's right to disconnect (FACT), New York's ban on notifications to minors from 12am to 6am, effective January 25, 2027 (FACT).

**Bridges:**

## Exhibit 09 · Babel or Pentecost

**Compression point:** *"Babel scattered the languages. Pentecost let each one hear in its own tongue. AI translation could become either."*

**Rooms (from the Babel dossier):**
1. **The Babel meter.** 7,164 living languages (FACT, Ethnologue), sized by speakers and colored by machine coverage, against 544 languages still waiting for Bible translation to begin, down from 985 a year earlier (FACT, Wycliffe Aug 2025). English is 42.56% of one Common Crawl snapshot's pages (FACT).
2. **Same question, many altars.** One grief question asked with faith unstated, then stated. When faith is unstated, Catholic and Sunni users get slightly misaligned counsel (FACT, FaithfulBench, Sept 2026); 59% of Americans expect religion in grief answers, and models provide it far less (REPORTED, Axios).
3. **Pentecost round-trip.** Untranslatable words (*hesed*, *dharma*, *taqwa*, *grace*, *dukkha*) round-tripped through machines: what survives, what flattens.
4. **Saints in the Machine.** (Planned room.) Chatbots that speak as saints, prophets or Jesus. It is the Second Death's question turned on the communion of saints: when the "voice" of a holy figure is a model, is that devotion, theater or idolatry? WATCH: build its case list from primary sources before drafting. Do not cite any installation or app from memory.

## Companion · Mechanism Brief · "HIPAA for the Pews"

**Format:** Mechanism Brief (Volume I · The Hidden Clocks), not an exhibit. A one-page standard plus a worksheet.

**Compression point:** *"The church already keeps a book of the names of the dead. It has no rule for the rest of what it keeps."* (Callback: The Second Death's Book of the Names of the Dead, REPORTED.)

**The clock:** Use a real date, not a slogan. Candidates: the January 25, 2027 effective date of New York's SAFE for Kids rules (relevant to youth-ministry apps), or the next All Saints, when many parishes open their book of names. Choose one at INVESTIGATE; do not invent a deadline.

**The standard (draft clauses, each sourced to an exhibit):**
1. **What we record at the door** and how long we keep it (The Quiet Guest).
2. **Who may watch our members online** and when we stop (Guardian Angel Inc.).
3. **How long a child's check-in record lives** (Check-In).
4. **What our counseling and prayer tools may store** (The Companion Confessor).
5. **What we refuse to infer** about members (Inferred Faith).
6. **How a member's digital remains are kept, and how they end** (The Second Death: consent, real words only, no data mining, a defined end).
7. **Who answers a subpoena, a buyer or a bankruptcy court** (When the Company Closes: if the church app vendor fails, where does the prayer list go?).

**Naming caution:** "HIPAA" is a statute that does not apply to most congregations. The page must say so in its first paragraph. The name is a working title borrowed for recognition; the final title may be *The Pew Standard*.

**Governance role:** This brief becomes the communal tab in EP 11's Lever Map and the Faith Layer's closing companion.

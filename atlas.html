What this atlas is and is not.

The Atlas covers 40 representative Georgia counties — the most populated, plus key examples for each pressure type. It is not all 159 counties. Expansion to full coverage is queued. Metric values shown range from documented and sourced (county population, FL license-surrender intake) to illustrative-of-pattern (composite affordability index, provisional rejection rate). Hovering each overlay button shows what tier of evidence backs that overlay.

The atlas is the geographic-intelligence companion to the Mechanism Map. The Map shows how pressures connect conceptually. The Atlas shows where they land physically. Together they answer the systems-view question the critique opened: where are the invisible forces actually operating, and what would it take to see them?

About the atlas

A six-overlay geographic-pressure map of Georgia covering 40 representative counties. Built as the Phase 3 geographic layer of the Infera Mechanism Series — sister page to the Map. Coverage will expand to all 159 counties as data per pressure is assembled.

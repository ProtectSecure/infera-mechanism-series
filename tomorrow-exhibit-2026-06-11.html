The Infera read: the truce is being tested at sea, not at the table. Section 702, the last clean day before the lights go out

T02 · FLOOR ACTION, TIME TBA · US SENATE
FACT (DEADLINE, VOTE RECORD) / WATCH (RETRY TIMING)

The surveillance authority lapses Friday June 12 without Senate action. Cloture failed 47 to 52 on June 5 with seven Republican defections, and Thune has promised another run this week while the Pulte acting-DNI fight entangles the count. The story is no longer reform versus reauthorize; it is lapse versus stopgap, with the DNI nomination as the hostage mechanism. An actual lapse would be the first 702 dark period since enactment.

84HIGH GRAVITY

NEXT CHECKPOINT: cloture re-file timing; any hotlined short-term extension; ODNI statements on collection continuity; House posture if a stopgap passes.

PPI asks how much more is coming

T04 · 8:30 AM · BUREAU OF LABOR STATISTICS
FACT (SCHEDULED) / WATCH (PRINT)

Yesterday's CPI ran 0.5 percent on the month and 4.2 percent on the year, with energy carrying more than 60 percent of the monthly gain. Today's producer prices show whether the pipeline pressure is still building or peaking. CPI answered how much; PPI answers how much more is coming. A hot energy and goods line says 4.2 is not the peak. The quiet branch is trade-services margins, the tariff burden-shift read.

78HIGH GRAVITY

The full committee marks up the FY2027 Defense bill the morning after a US tanker engagement in the Gulf, following a closed subcommittee markup on June 10. A defense money markup during a live naval confrontation is where war policy becomes line items. Watch for Gulf supplemental language and any Hormuz-contingency funding.

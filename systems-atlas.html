Season III · Provisional map v3 · in planning

Four questions, six episodes, every seed already on the page.

Season III asks what the worst case is, what the best case is, who is managing this, and what is actually possible now. Version 3 draws on the finished Faith Layer, where a congregation turned out to be the sharpest test of every question: the smallest institution holding the most intimate data under the fewest rules. Each episode opens on a seed case a returning reader will recognize, and each is built for a 15 to 25 minute read.

1

The most intimate records sit outside every privacy law. HIPAA reaches only health plans, clearinghouses and providers who bill electronically.

2

Privilege covers the words, not the file. Courts keep ordering church records produced; a chatbot log has no privilege at all.

3

People cannot see their own hours. Self-reported and logged screen time correlate only moderately, and the errors run both ways.

4

Nobody is the integrator. None of the seven standards in the main evangelical accreditation code mentions data, and no one has counted congregations' written privacy policies.

Sources for each finding are in Exhibits 02, 06 and 07 of the Faith Layer. Season III is provisional: titles, order and seeds can change, and nothing here is published reporting until the episode is.

What changed

From a diagram to a map you can walk.

The earlier atlas drew Season I and Season II as two clusters joined through the substrate, with a pink spine of Season II mechanisms explaining Season I cases. Every one of those connections is still here, in the same evidence styles. This version adds the desk, the Faith Layer and Season III, and lays the lanes out left to right so connections can be read one at a time.

The Substrate

The shared middle.

Both seasons run through the same four platforms (Meta, Google, Apple, Amazon) and a broker tier (Acxiom, Experian, LexisNexis, CoreLogic). That shared layer is what makes the seasons one publication.

Forecasts

Dashed and dotted lines are not facts.

The Trust Market to Personhood feedback remains speculative. Season III seed lines are editorial plans. If a forecast materializes we relabel it; if a plan changes we redraw it.

Archived

Two exhibits held for later.

The Pulpit Feed and Babel or Pentecost stay online but sit outside the seven-exhibit Faith Layer. Their material is held for a future piece that joins them, and is not drawn on this map.
